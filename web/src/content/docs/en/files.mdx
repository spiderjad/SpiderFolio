## Spaces

All content lives in **spaces**: each one is the root of a tree of folders, pages and files. You'll find them under **Files** in the side menu.

<Shot name="espacios" alt="The list of spaces with favorites, recent items and the reason for each access" />

- A space can belong to **the company** or to **a department**. In a department's space, its head and deputies manage it, and the department's people get in with the level you choose: **Can edit** or **Can only view**.
- The list has **Favorites** (the star), **Recent** items, filters (**All**, **My departments**, **Favorites**, **Private**), sorting by name, department or date, and a **Cards** or **List** view. More items load as you reach the end.
- Each space tells you **why you have access**: "Head of Finance", "Deputy of Finance (parent area)", "Member of Accounting · includes sub-areas", "Admin", "Access given to you".
- **New space** (if you have permission) asks for a name, description, emoji, color, department and visibility.

## Who sees each space

There are three visibility levels:

- **Normal**: the whole team sees it (read-only, unless someone has more access).
- **Private**: only people who were given access, the department's people and admins can see it. It's marked with a padlock.
- **Confidential**: on top of that, **admins can't get in on their own**. Only the workspace owner, the department's head and deputy (and those of parent areas), its members and anyone added explicitly. It's marked with another padlock and the word "Confidential".

In a department space, **Include subareas** (off by default) extends members' access to the sub-departments. Heads and deputies of parent departments can always manage the spaces of their sub-areas.

**Members**, in the space's actions, gives access to departments (recommended: when someone changes teams, their access follows) or to people, with a level (**View**, **Comment**, **Edit**, **Manage**) and an optional expiry date. Readers and guests never go beyond **Comment**. It works just like sharing an item: see [Sharing](/en/docs/sharing).

## Folders and files

When you open a space you see the explorer: the folder tree on one side and the folder's contents on the other, with the path (breadcrumbs) at the top and **Your access** with its reason.

<Shot name="explorador" alt="A space's explorer with the folder tree, the file list and the New menu" />

- **New** creates a **New folder** or a **New page** (see [Pages](/en/docs/pages)), and lets you **Upload files**, **Upload folder** or **Import ZIP**. You can also **drag files and folders** from your computer. Large files are uploaded in parts and resume if interrupted.
- The file's real type is checked, not just its extension. Executables, HTML and SVG are not allowed.
- If a file with that name already exists, you choose **Replace (new version)**, **Keep both** or **Skip**.
- **View** opens a preview without downloading. **Download** saves the file.
- The list filters by type (**All**, **Pages**, **Files**), supports **multiple selection**, sorting and search, and shows who modified each item and the total size of each folder.

## Versions

Each file keeps its versions. In the file's menu, **Versions** shows the history: you can **Upload a new version**, download any of them, or click **Restore this one** to make an earlier one current again. If the file is a controlled document, its versions follow the rules in [Controlled documents](/en/docs/controlled-documents).

## Import a ZIP

**Import ZIP** (or **Import ZIP contents** in the menu of a ZIP already uploaded) extracts it keeping its folders, optionally into a new folder. If the ZIP contains a single folder at the root, its contents go straight in, without repeating a level. Anything that can't be imported is skipped and explained at the end. Afterwards you can **Delete the original ZIP** to free up space.

## Move

Moving works like Google Drive, in any of these ways:

- **Move**, in the item's menu or the selection bar: a dialog with the folder tree, where you can change spaces and create a new folder. Finish with **Move here**.
- **Drag and drop** onto a folder in the list, the tree or the path.
- **Cut and paste**: Ctrl+X cuts the selection and Ctrl+V pastes it into the folder you're viewing.

You need to be able to **edit** both the source and the destination. A folder can't be moved into itself. If an item with that name already exists, you choose **Replace** or **Keep both**. When it's done you can **Undo** or **Go to folder**.

## Download as ZIP

Select files and folders and click **Download ZIP** (or choose it in a folder's menu).

- Up to **500 MB**: the download starts right away. The link is single-use and lasts a few minutes; your permissions are checked again when it's used.
- Over 500 MB: it's prepared in the background and you get an **email with a temporary link** (7 days). It doesn't count toward your storage.
- Only what you can see is included.

## Shared

**Shared**, in the side menu, has two tabs: **With me** (what was shared with you in spaces where you don't have full access, with new items marked) and **By me** (the access you gave, so you can remove it or change its expiry). See [Sharing](/en/docs/sharing).

## Trash

**Move to trash** sends the item (and, for a folder, everything in it) to the space's **Trash**. It's kept there for **30 days** and can be **Restored**. **Delete** removes it permanently, with all its versions; after 30 days it's deleted automatically.
