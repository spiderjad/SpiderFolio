SpiderFolio is open source and works fully without plugins. Plugins add features for the companies that
need them (for example, the [Corporate directory](/en/docs/corporate-directory) or the
[Connector for Claude](/en/docs/connector-for-claude)). Some are **free** and others **paid**: paid ones work
with your company's license. The catalog is on the [Plugins](/en/plugins) page.

## Where it is

In **Administration › Plugins › License and plugins**. Only the company's **owner** sees it. The screens of
the active plugins show up in that same **Plugins** group of the menu.

<Shot name="licencia" alt="The License and plugins screen: the list of plugins with their license and expiry, the Renew or change license, Turn on and Guide buttons, and the company ID at the bottom" />

It's a single list with every plugin on the server. Each row shows:

- **Its name, version and type**: **Paid**, **Free** or **Unverified** (third-party).
- **Its state**: **Active**, **Off**, **No license**, **License expired**, **Replaced by…**,
  **Loads on restart** or **Removed on restart**.
- **For paid ones, its license**: "License LIC-2026-0003 · expires 31 Dec 2027". With 30 days or fewer left
  it says "expires in 12 days", highlighted, and once expired, "expired…".
- **The buttons** for what can be done with that plugin: **Guide**, **Renew or change license** (or **Load
  license**), **Turn on** or **Turn off**, and **Remove** under **⋯**.

With 8 plugins or more, a search box appears at the top.

## Renew or change the license

Your company has **a single license**, covering all its paid plugins. When you renew or add a plugin we send
you a new license that includes them all.

1. In the row of any paid plugin, click **Renew or change license** (if it has none yet, **Load license**).
2. Paste the full text we sent you: it starts with `SFL1.`.
3. Click **Save license**.

You can also upload the `.sfplugin` we sent you (see below): the license is inside and loads on its own.

The license is checked on your own server, offline. An invalid, expired or another company's license is
rejected. If the loaded one stops being valid, a notice above the list says so.

## The company ID

Below the list, in small print, is the **Company ID**. Click **Copy** and send it to us when you ask for a
license: we issue it for that ID and it only works in your company.

## Turn on a free plugin

**Free** plugins are signed by SpiderJAD and need no license. Click **Turn on** or **Turn off** in their row.
An **Unverified** one (third-party) asks you to confirm you trust whoever made it, since it runs inside your
server.

A plugin **Replaced by…** isn't turned on: another active plugin already does the same and uses its data
(for example, the Corporate directory includes [Sign in with Microsoft](/en/docs/sign-in-with-microsoft)).
Its row only shows the guide. If the other one stops being active, this one comes back on its own, with
everything as it was.

## Upload a plugin

A `.sfplugin` file is installed from this screen, without going into the server. It's always signed by
SpiderJAD: for a paid plugin we send it to you with your company's license; for a free one you download it
from the [plugin catalog](/en/plugins) and it's turned on in your company when you upload it.

1. Click **Upload plugin** and choose the file. You need two-step verification turned on (see
   [Security and two-step verification](/en/docs/security-and-2fa)); if you confirmed it more than a few
   minutes ago, it asks for the code.
2. The server checks the signature, that nothing was modified and that the license is your company's, and
   installs it. If anything is off, it tells you why and installs nothing.
3. The plugin shows **Loads on restart**. In the notice at the top click **Restart now** and confirm with
   **Restart in 1 minute**.

During that minute everyone signed in sees a countdown notice so they save what they're editing. The app comes
back on its own in a few seconds, with the plugin loaded. If the server doesn't restart on its own, the notice
says so: whoever runs it restarts the app and the worker.

## Remove a plugin

1. In the plugin's row, click **⋯ › Remove**.
2. Read what happens and confirm with **Remove from the server**:
   - It stops loading on the **next restart**, scheduled on its own in 1 minute, warning whoever is signed in.
     If the server doesn't restart on its own, whoever runs it restarts the app and the worker.
   - **Its data and settings are kept**: if you upload it again, it comes back as it was.
   - For paid ones, **the license doesn't change**.
   - If the server has several companies, it's removed for all of them.

Only plugins uploaded from the app can be removed. Those built into the server image go with the image.

Uploading, removing, loading the license and restarting go to **Administration › Audit log** (never the
license text).

## If the license expires

Thirty days before, each paid plugin's row warns "expires in N days". Once it expires, those plugins stop
applying and SpiderFolio keeps working the same. Their settings are kept: when you load a new license they
apply again exactly as they were.

To remove the company's license, open **Renew or change license** and click **Remove the license**.

## Plugins copied onto the server

Whoever runs the server can also install a plugin without the app: copy its folder into the plugins volume and
restart the app and the worker (from the `deploy/` folder):

```sh
docker compose cp ./my-plugin app:/data/plugins/
docker compose restart app worker
```

Its migrations run on their own at startup. An active plugin can add a screen in the **Plugins** group of
Administration and new capabilities in [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions).
