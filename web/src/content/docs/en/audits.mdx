## What it's for

IFS Food 8 and BRCGS 9 ask for a **yearly internal audit programme** covering the whole standard, and for every
finding, internal or external, to have its correction and corrective action with evidence. **Audits** (Quality
menu) holds the year's audits, the standard's coverage and each one's findings, which end in their
[nonconformity](/en/docs/nonconformities).

## The year's programme

The screen shows the chosen year (top right) with its audits by date: the type, the auditor, the result, its
findings and how many nonconformities are still open, and its status: **Planned**, **Done** or **Cancelled**.

**Internal programme coverage**: for each standard the company is audited against (chosen in
**Administration** › **Security** › **General**, **Food quality** card), its chapters and whether a **done**
internal audit covered them this year. The unmarked chapters are the ones still to audit.

## Plan an audit

Whoever manages quality clicks **New audit** and fills in:

- the **type**: **Internal**, **IFS Food 8**, **BRCGS Food Safety 9**, **Customer**, **Official inspection**
  or **Other external**;
- the title, the scope and the **planned date**;
- the **auditor** (a person or a body) and the **owner in the company**, who leads it;
- for an internal one, the **chapters it covers** of the company's standards;
- for an external one, if you like, the **audit room** so the auditor sees the evidence (see
  [Audit rooms](/en/docs/audit-rooms)).

The owner is told 7 days before, the day before and on the day, and sees it in [Expiries](/en/docs/expiries).

## Findings

On the audit's record, **Add finding** asks for the **clause** (for example, 4.12.1), the finding and the
**severity** (minor, major or critical; an IFS KO is entered as critical). With **Open its nonconformity**
ticked (it is by default), the NC is created:

- with origin **Internal audit** or **External audit**, as the audit is;
- with the finding's severity and the title “Clause 4.12.1: …”;
- in the chosen owner's hands (by default, whoever leads the audit), who gets a notice.

From there it follows its path: root cause, actions and check. If you didn't open it when recording the
finding, **Open nonconformity** next to it creates it later. The NC's record links its audit.

## Close or cancel

- **Close the audit** asks for the date it was done and, if you like, the **result** (for example, “Higher
  level, 96.3 %”). A done audit still takes findings, but it's no longer edited.
- **Cancel** asks for a reason; it stays in the programme as cancelled.

## Who sees what

| Who                    | What they see and do                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality**     | Everything: plans, edits, records findings, closes and cancels.        |
| **View quality**       | Reads the programme, the coverage and each audit.                      |
| Whoever leads an audit | Sees it even without capabilities, records its findings and closes it. |

Linking an audit room requires seeing it: you created it or you're the owner.
