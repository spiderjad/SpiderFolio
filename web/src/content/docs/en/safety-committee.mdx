## What it's for

The **Committee** section brings together the workers' **consultation and participation**: the **prevention
delegates** and the **health and safety committee** (in Latin America, the **joint committee**, with the
workers' and the employer's representatives), as articles 35 to 38 of Spain's Law 31/1995 require.

## Members

Whoever manages the area adds each person with their **role** and the dates of their **term**:

- prevention delegate;
- company representative;
- prevention technician, as an adviser;
- secretary.

Before a delegate's term ends (two months, a month, a week and on the day), whoever manages the area is told.
**Remove from the committee** keeps the person in the history; **Show former members** shows them.

## Meetings

1. In the **Meetings** tab, click **Record a meeting**.
2. Note the date, whether it's **ordinary** or **extraordinary**, who attended and the agenda (one item per
   line).
3. Save.

The committee meets at least once a quarter: three months after the last ordinary meeting, whoever manages the
area is told (in [Expiries](/en/docs/expiries)). The list filters by type and dates.

## Minutes and agreements

- **Minutes**: on the meeting, **Create the minutes from the template** creates a page in the Health & safety
  space with the date, type, attendees and agenda already written, to finish there. You can also pick a page or
  file that's already in that space when you record or edit the meeting.
- **Agreements**: **New agreement** creates a [planning](/en/docs/preventive-planning) measure linked to the
  meeting (origin “Committee”), with its owner and due date.

## What a committee member sees

Committee members see the **Committee** section in the Health & safety menu even if they don't manage the area:
the members, meetings, minutes and agreements, read only, without any other Health & safety data. The minutes
open with the Health & safety space's permissions (the area creates it visible to the whole company unless
changed).
