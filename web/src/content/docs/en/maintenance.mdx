## What it's for

The **Maintenance** module keeps the plant's **equipment**, the **breakdowns** anyone reports (from a phone, a
tablet or the QR stuck on the machine, typing or talking), the **work orders** with their time, spare parts
and **hygiene release**, **preventive** maintenance and **calibration**, and the **indicators**
(availability, MTBF, MTTR). It's built for the food industry: what IFS, BRCGS and a health inspection ask for.

## Turning it on

In **Administration › Security › General**, the **Maintenance** card. Only the **owner**. Turning it on adds
the **Maintenance** area to the bar and creates its **documents space**, where each piece of equipment has its
folder (manuals, diagrams, certificates, photos).

Turning it off hides the area and pauses the notices. **It deletes nothing.**

## Who does what

Three capabilities, in **Administration › Roles and permissions**:

- **Manage maintenance**: equipment, plans, creating, assigning, closing and cancelling work orders. By
  default, owner and administrators.
- **Work on maintenance**: for technicians. They see every work order, take them, log time and spare parts
  and mark them as done.
- **View maintenance**: reads everything without changing anything (management, quality).

**Anyone** but guests can **report a breakdown**: reader seats and the plant tablets' **username + PIN**
accounts too. Whoever only reports sees their reports and where they stand.

## Equipment

In **Maintenance › Equipment**, lines, machines and their components as a tree («Line 1 › Filler 2»), with
their location, manufacturer, model, serial number and **criticality** (A, B or C). Mark **In the product
zone** the ones that touch food: their work orders need the hygiene release before closing.

Each piece of equipment has its **QR** to stick on the machine: scanning it opens the report with it already
chosen. And its **Documents** folder.

## Reporting a breakdown

From **Maintenance › Report a breakdown**, from the equipment's QR or, on a username + PIN tablet, from the
big card on its home. Choose the equipment, tell what's going on and whether it stops production, and
maintenance gets it right away. An urgent report notifies whoever manages maintenance.

**By talking**: with [Bian](/en/docs/bian) on, a **Record** button shows up. The person tells what's going on
(«filler two is losing pressure and the line is stopped») and the AI fills in the report: title, equipment,
description and priority with its reason. It's checked and sent; **nothing is created without confirming**.

<Shot
  name="mantenimiento-avisar"

Audio is turned into text on the server itself with Whisper (see [Configuration](/en/docs/configuration),
`WHISPER_URL`); without it, with the browser's dictation. The audio is kept 30 days and then deleted; the text
stays on the work order.

## Work orders

In **Maintenance › Work orders**, all of them, filtered by status, priority, type and equipment; the open ones
first by urgency. **My work orders** are the ones assigned to each technician.

<Shot
  name="mantenimiento-ordenes"
  alt="Open work orders: the filler's urgent one in progress, the clipper reported and the lighting improvement planned"
/>

The cycle: **Reported** → **Planned** (with technician and date) → **In progress** → **Done** → **Closed**. On
the work order:

- **I'll take it**, **Start** and **Stop timer**: each technician's time is logged by itself; it can be logged
  by hand too.
- **Add spare part**: searching the warehouse's catalogue (with its stock) or typing it.
- **Lockout/tagout (LOTO)**: if the work order needs it, it warns before starting.
- **Mark as done**: cause, what was done and downtime minutes. Or **Close by talking**: the technician tells
  what they found and did and the AI proposes cause, solution, time and spare parts.
- **Hygiene release**: on equipment in the product zone, quality confirms it was left clean, with no tools or
  debris (IFS 4.17.4), and only then can it be **Closed**.

<Shot
  name="mantenimiento-orden"
  alt="An urgent work order in progress: the equipment, the lockout warning, the time, the spare parts and the history"
/>

**Suggest a resolution**: on a breakdown, the AI reads the symptom, the resolved breakdowns of the same
equipment and of the ones of its same model, and the manuals it finds, and proposes the likely causes with
their steps, **citing where it gets them from**. It's a suggestion: the technician decides and says whether it
helped.

## Preventive and calibration

In **Maintenance › Preventive**, each piece of equipment's plans: **by time** (every N days, weeks or months)
or **by use** (every N hours or cycles of a meter, in the **Meters** tab). Each plan has its tasks, its
technician, the estimated minutes and how many days ahead the work order shows up.

<Shot
  name="mantenimiento-preventivo"
  alt="Preventive and calibration plans: greasing the filler every two weeks, the overhaul by operating hours and the calibrations of the scale and the probes"
/>

When it's due, **the work order is created by itself**, with its tasks to tick. Done, the next one counts from
that day (or that reading); cancelled, that one is skipped.

**Calibration** plans (probes, scales, metal detectors) also show on **Quality › Prerequisites**, without
being entered twice.

## Link with Quality

If a monitoring record is out of limits (the cold room at 9 °C, the dryer off its curve) and opens a
nonconformity, and the record names a piece of equipment («Cold room 1 · carcasses» names the equipment «Cold
room 1»), an **urgent work order** is created on that equipment, linked to the nonconformity. From the
nonconformity it can also be **asked of maintenance**. The nonconformity shows how its work order is going and
its owner is notified when it's finished.

## Spare parts and warehouse stock (SGA)

The stock belongs to the warehouse system: Maintenance reads it and tells it what was used. In the module's
card, **Warehouse integration (SGA)**, the owner creates a **token** (shown only once) and the SGA uses it with
the API:

- `PUT /api/v1/integrations/maintenance/parts`: the catalogue (code, description, unit, stock, minimum and
  location), up to 2000 per call, in UTF-8.
- `GET /api/v1/integrations/maintenance/consumption?since=…`: the spare parts used on the work orders, to
  discount them.

Adding a spare part to a work order searches that catalogue, with its stock and a warning when it reaches the
minimum.

## Summary

**Maintenance › Summary**, by period and for the whole plant or a line or machine: **availability**, **mean
time between failures** (MTBF), **mean time to repair** (MTTR), downtime hours, preventive versus corrective,
**preventive compliance** and open work orders, and the equipment with the most breakdowns. **Export CSV** gives
each piece of equipment's.
