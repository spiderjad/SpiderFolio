## What it's for

IFS Food 8 (4.18) and BRCGS 9 (3.9) ask you to follow a lot from the raw material, and its supplier, to the
customer, and back, quickly, and to test it once a year. In the Quality area, **Traceability** brings together
the lots, which lot went into which and what went out to each customer.

<Shot
  name="calidad-trazabilidad"
  alt="The list of lots: raw materials with their supplier, the mix and the finished product lots"
/>

Each lot has a **type**: raw material, intermediate or finished product. Search by the lot code, the supplier
lot or the material, and filter by type.

## Loading the lots

There are two ways, and they can be combined:

- **By hand**: **New lot** asks for the type, the code (the same as in the ERP, the MES or the label), the
  product or material and, for a raw material, the **supplier**, the **supplier lot** and the **origin**
  (slaughterhouse, country). On the lot's page, **Add input lot** says which lot went into this one, and **Add
  shipment** records which customer it went out to, with its delivery note.
- **Importing a CSV**: **Import** takes three files, one at a time: first the **lots**, then the **links**
  (which lot went into which) and last the **shipments**. Each has its **template**. **Check** goes through
  the file row by row and says how many rows are new, how many are updated and which have errors and why;
  **Import** writes the valid ones. A lot of the same type and code is updated, and an empty cell doesn't
  erase what it already has.

A link that would make a lot be made of itself is refused.

## Tracing

Each lot's page shows its data and its **trace**:

- **Backward**: which lots it comes from, down to the raw materials, with their supplier and lot.
- **Forward**: which lots it went into, up to the finished products, and their **shipments**: to which
  customers, with which delivery note and how much.

<Shot
  name="calidad-lote"
  alt="The forward trace of a raw material: the mix, the two chorizo lots and their customers"
/>

At the top are the totals (lots, raw materials and suppliers, or customers and quantity shipped) and how long
the query took. **Export CSV** and **Export PDF** produce the report to send to the food authority or a
customer; the export is recorded in the audit log.

Below, the page lists what the lot comes from, what it went into, its shipments and what mentions it: product
on hold, complaints and recall tests.

## Recall test

Whoever manages quality can click **Record as a recall test** on the trace: it opens the recall test with the
product, the lot, the direction and the **minutes** since they opened the trace. Only the % recovered and the
result are left (see [Complaints and recall](/en/docs/complaints-and-recall)).

## With the rest of Quality

When recording **product on hold** or a **complaint**, whoever views quality can pick the **Traceability
lot**: the code stays as usual and the lot's page shows them.

## Who sees what

| Who                | What they see and do                                             |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality** | Everything: loads lots, links and shipments, imports and traces. |
| **View quality**   | Sees the lots, traces and exports.                               |
| Everyone else      | No access: customers and quantities are commercial data.         |
