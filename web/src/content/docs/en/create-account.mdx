## Sign up

1. Open your installation's address followed by `/register` (for example, `https://docs.your-company.com/register`).
2. Fill in **Full name**, **Company name**, **Email** and **Password**. The password needs at least 10 characters.
3. Click **Create account**.

The screen appears in your browser's language; you can change it with the **ES / EN** switch at the top right. The language you sign up in becomes your account's language and the company's default language (you can change them later in **My profile** and in the administration).

Whoever signs up creates the company and becomes its **owner**. If you haven't installed SpiderFolio yet, start with [Install on your server](/en/docs/install).

## Confirm your email

We send you an email with a link. When you open it you'll see "Done, your email is confirmed." and a button to go to the app.

Until you confirm your email, Home shows a notice: "Confirm your email to invite your team and publish documents." If the email didn't arrive, click **Resend email** in that notice.

If the link isn't valid or has expired, sign in and request a new one with **Send a new link**.
