## What it's for

**Quality** is the app's area for a food business's food safety: what **IFS Food version 8**, **BRCGS Food
Safety Issue 9** and the official health inspection ask for. It builds on what you already use in the rest
of the app: [controlled documents](/en/docs/controlled-documents), [forms and records](/en/docs/forms),
[expiries](/en/docs/expiries) and [audit rooms](/en/docs/audit-rooms).

Today it brings:

- **[nonconformities and their CAPA](/en/docs/nonconformities)**: everything that goes wrong, with its cause,
  its corrective and preventive actions and the check that it was solved;
- the **[HACCP plan and product on hold](/en/docs/haccp-plan)**: the critical control points table linked to
  their records, the meat template and the signed decision on every lot on hold;
- **[audits](/en/docs/audits)**: the year's programme with its coverage by chapter of the standard, and the
  findings by clause that open their nonconformity;
- **[suppliers](/en/docs/suppliers)**: their risk and fraud vulnerability, their certificates and technical
  sheets with expiry, their yearly review and their nonconformities;
- **[complaints and recall](/en/docs/complaints-and-recall)**: every complaint with its deadline and its
  answer, complaints per million units, the crisis team and contacts and the recall tests.

## Turn it on

The area is off by default. The owner turns it on in **Administration** › **Security** › **General**, on the
**Food quality** card:

1. Switch on **Turn on the quality module**.
2. Tick the **Standards the company is audited against**: IFS Food 8, BRCGS Food Safety 9, both or neither.
   Today they're only a reference; later they'll order the audits' clauses.
3. If you like, change the **Days until the effectiveness check** (30 by default): from the last action done
   to the date proposed to check the problem didn't come back.

Turning it on creates the **Quality** space, shown in the area, for documents, records and evidence. Turning
it off hides the area and its notices, without deleting anything.

## How it's organized

With the module on, **Quality** shows in the **areas rail** (see
[Home](/en/docs/home#getting-around-the-areas)). The area's menu has:

- **Summary**, **Nonconformities**, **Actions**, **HACCP plan**, **Product on hold**, **Audits**, **Suppliers**, **Complaints**, **Crisis and recall**, **Prerequisites**, **Sampling plan** and **Products**, for whoever sees
  them all;
- **Mine**: **My actions**, **Reported by me** and **Report a nonconformity** (and, for whoever doesn't see
  them all, **Record a complaint**, **My controls** and **My samples**);
- the area's spaces, such as **Quality**.

The **Summary** is the module's dashboard: open nonconformities by severity and origin, overdue actions, checks
due today and the average days to close; the CCP records and those out of limits in the last 30 days and the
product on hold still undecided; suppliers' expired or expiring certificates and overdue reviews; open and
overdue complaints and the year's complaints per million; the audits of the next 60 days with the chapter
coverage; the overdue or non-conforming [prerequisites](/en/docs/prerequisites); the overdue samples of the
[sampling plan](/en/docs/sampling-plan) and the non-conforming results of 30 days, and the outdated or
in-review [labels](/en/docs/products-and-labels) and the ready-to-eat products without a shelf life study.

## Who sees what

| Who                     | What they see and do                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality**      | Everything: creates, analyses, assigns, checks, closes, cancels and reopens. Changes the form settings. The Admin role already has it. |
| **View quality**        | Reads every nonconformity and its actions, and exports the CSV. Meant for management or an audit.                                      |
| A nonconformity's owner | Handles it: details, root cause, actions and check date. Doesn't close it.                                                             |
| An action's responsible | Sees its nonconformity and marks their action done.                                                                                    |
| Heads and deputies      | Read the nonconformities assigned to their department (and sub-departments).                                                           |
| Everyone                | **Report a nonconformity** and see the ones they reported.                                                                             |

The capabilities are given in [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions).
