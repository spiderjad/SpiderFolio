**Members** is the list of everyone in your company: their role, whether they take a full or a reader seat,
which departments they belong to and when they last signed in. From here you also invite people, change
roles, suspend and remove people.

## Where to find it

In the side menu, click **Administration** and then **Members**. You can also get there from the company
menu (top left), **Company settings**.

You need the **View members** or **Manage members** capability. If you have neither, the section doesn't
appear.

If you're the head or deputy of a department, the screen is called **My team** and shows only the people in
the departments you lead and their sub-areas.

## List and Org chart

At the top you choose the view:

- **List**: the table with everyone (the usual view).
- **Org chart**: the company as a tree, with the owner at the top and every department and sub-area below.
  Each area opens to show its head, deputies and members. It has a search box (**Search for a person in
  the org chart**) and the **Expand all** and **Collapse all** buttons. People who aren't in any department
  appear at the end, under **No department**.

Above the org chart is the **Structure review**, which flags what's worth fixing: departments without a
head, people without a department, heads with a reader seat and departments with no people. Admins who are
also heads show up as **For your information**: nothing needs fixing.

If you can manage the person, the org chart gives you **Make head**, **Make deputy**, **Remove position**
and **Move to another department**. More in [Departments](/en/docs/departments).

## Summary and seats

In the **List** view, at the very top you see the **Seats**: how many **Full users** and **Readers** there
are. There's no limit; pending invitations also take up a seat and suspended people don't.

- **Full users** create, edit and administer.
- **Readers** read and confirm reads. External guests and people who sign in with a PIN are readers too.

Below is the **Summary by hierarchy**: **All**, **Owner**, **Admins**, **Department heads**, **Members**
and **Readers**, with how many people are in each group. Click one to filter the table and click it again to
clear the filter.

## The table

Each row is a person:

- **Name**: their name, email (or username, if they sign in with a PIN) and job title. Your own row shows
  **You**.
- **Role**: what they can administer. If you can change it, it's a selector and saves instantly; it only
  offers the roles you can give.
- **Departments**: the ones they belong to.
- **Seat**: **Full** or **Reader**.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Suspended**. If a PIN user got it wrong too many times, you'll also see
  **PIN locked**.
- **2FA**: **On** if they use two-step verification.
- **Last seen**: the last time they used the company, or **Never**.

To find someone, type in the search box (**Search by name, email or job title**) or use the **Role** (it
includes a **Department heads** option), **Department** and **Status** filters. Click a column title to
sort. The list loads more people as you reach the end, and the filters stay in the page address, so you can
bookmark or share it.

## Change a person

Open the row's menu (the three dots). Each option only appears if you have the capability it needs:

| Option | What it does | Capability |
|---|---|---|
| **Edit** | Changes **Full name**, **Role**, **Seat**, **Job title** and **Departments**. | **Manage members** |
| **Suspend** / **Reactivate** | While suspended, they lose access right away and stop taking up a seat. You can undo it from the notice or reactivate them anytime. | **Suspend members** |
| **Reset PIN** | PIN users only: gives them a new PIN, closes their sessions and unlocks them. | **Manage PIN sign-in** |
| **Reset two-step verification** | For someone who lost their phone and codes. See [Security and two-step verification](/en/docs/security-and-2fa). | **Manage members** |
| **Sign out their sessions** | Closes all their sessions, including in other companies on their account. | **Suspend members** |
| **Remove from workspace** | They no longer belong to the company and lose all access. Their account isn't deleted, but to come back they need a new invitation. | **Manage members** |

Every sensitive action asks for confirmation.

In **Edit**, the email can only be changed if the person hasn't confirmed it yet: they'll have to confirm the
new one and their sessions are closed. If they already confirmed it, they change it themselves from **My
profile**.

## Bulk changes

Tick several rows and a bar appears with **Change role**, **Change seat**, **Departments** (**Add to
departments**, **Remove from departments** or **Replace departments**), **Suspend** and **Reactivate**. To
suspend in bulk you type the word "suspend" in the confirmation.

Each person goes through the same rules as if you changed them one by one. If some can't be changed, at the
end we tell you how many were updated and how many weren't.

## Export the list

Click **Export CSV**, at the top right. You get a file with the people you see according to your current
filters and sort order: name, email, job title, role, seat, status, departments, last seen and join date. It
opens fine in Excel and the export is recorded in the audit log.

## People who sign in with a PIN

For people without an email (for example, shop-floor operators who share a tablet), click **Create person
with PIN**. You need the **Manage PIN sign-in** capability.

1. Enter the **Full name** and the **Username** (3 to 32 lowercase letters, numbers, periods, hyphens or
   underscores).
2. Choose the **Role** (only member roles without admin capabilities) and the **Departments**.
3. If you like, enter the 6-digit **PIN**. If you leave it empty, we generate one.
4. Click **Create**. The PIN is shown **only once**: hand it over in person. If it's lost, use **Reset PIN**.

At the bottom of the page, the **Sign-in with username and PIN** card lets you turn this sign-in on or off,
sign people out after inactivity or after they confirm a read, and copy the **Address for tablets**.

## Rules worth knowing

- You can only give roles that don't grant more power than you have.
- An admin doesn't manage other admins or the owner, and the owner role isn't assigned from here. On those
  rows the menu offers no actions.
- Heads and deputies don't change roles, seats or status: they add and remove people in their areas from
  **Departments**.
- There's always at least one active owner.
- You can't suspend or remove your own account.
- An external guest is always a **Reader** and doesn't join departments. A role with admin capabilities
  needs a **Full** seat.
