## Two kinds of search

- **Quick search** (Ctrl+K): finds a document, folder, space or person by name or code, and offers shortcuts.
- **Full-text search**: looks for a word or phrase inside the content of pages, PDFs and Word files.

Both show **only what you have permission to view**. What you can't see doesn't appear, not even its name.

## Quick search

Click **Search** in the top bar, or press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), from any screen.

1. Type part of the name, or the code of a controlled document (for example, PRC-004).
2. Results are grouped into **Documents**, **Folders**, **Spaces** and **People**. Before you type anything, you'll see your **Recent** items.
3. Move with ↑ ↓ and press Enter to open.

Under **Actions** you also have **New page**, **Go to …** (the app's sections) and **Ask Bian**, which passes what you typed to [Bian](/en/docs/bian).

To search inside the content, choose **Search for “…” in content**.

## Full-text search

This opens the **Search** screen, which looks in titles, codes and the content of pages, PDFs and Word documents (DOCX).

<Shot name="busqueda" alt="The Search screen with results and the search term highlighted in each passage" />

- Type at least two letters.
- Accents don't matter: "café" and "cafe" find the same results.
- Use **quotes** for an exact phrase: `"hand washing"`.
- Use a **hyphen** to exclude a word: `cleaning -weekly`.

Each result shows the name, the space it's in and a passage with the word highlighted. They're sorted by relevance, and more load as you reach the end ("Showing … (there are more)").

## Good to know

- The text of a newly uploaded PDF or Word file is extracted in the background: it can take a moment to show up in search.
- A scanned PDF (an image, with no text) is found by its name, but not by its content.
- Expired access and stopped inheritance are always respected: if you can no longer see something, it no longer appears.
- If you can't find anything, try another word, or ask Bian in your own words.
