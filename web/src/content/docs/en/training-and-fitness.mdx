## Training

A **course** is a [controlled document](/en/docs/controlled-documents): the saw procedure, the emergency plan,
food handling. That way training is always on the version in force.

**Create a course.** In **Training**, whoever manages the area clicks **New course**, chooses the document and,
if they like, its **Valid for months**.

- **Who has to do it** comes from its [required reading](/en/docs/required-reading): departments,
  **positions** or people.
- The **“I have read and understood”** of the current version is the **record**, with its date. With a
  validity, it expires: the person is told 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day, and the course shows
  **Confirm I did it again**.
- Each course shows every person with their state (**Up to date**, **Due soon**, **Expired**, **Pending**),
  and each person's sheet shows their training.

### In-person training with signatures

1. On the course, click **Record in-person training**.
2. Choose the **Attendees** and the **Date**.
3. Each attendee signs in their box with a finger (for example, passing the tablet around).

**Attendance record PDF** downloads that day's sheet with every signature; it needs
[Gotenberg](/en/docs/gotenberg).

### Let them sign on their phones

Instead of passing the tablet around, with the attendees and the date chosen click **Let them sign on their
phones**:

- It shows a **QR code** (and **Copy the link**) for that signing.
- Each attendee scans it, signs in with their username (or **Sign in with PIN**), sees the course, the date
  and who gave it, and signs with a finger.
- Only the people on the list can sign, each for themselves and once.
- Whoever gave it sees live who signed and who's missing, and can **Close the signing** whenever they want;
  otherwise it closes on its own after 24 hours. **Show the QR code** shows it again while it's open.

The signatures go into the same day's **Attendance record PDF**, wherever they were signed.

## Risk assessments

Turning the area on creates the **Risk assessment** document type (EVR, reviewed every 12 months). Upload your
assessments as controlled documents of that type:

- When an EVR is published, its review date is told to the heads and deputies of its owner department (or
  whoever made it, without one) 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day.
- From an EVR's controlled document window, **New preventive measure** creates a
  [planning](/en/docs/preventive-planning) measure tied to that assessment.

## Fitness for work

**Fitness for work** says whether a person can do their position's tasks, according to the medical check-up.
On each person's sheet, whoever has **Health data: accidents and fitness for work** clicks **Record fitness**:

- **Fit**, **Fit with restrictions** (with the tasks they can't do, never the causes) or **Not fit**;
- and until when it's valid.

Medical data is never stored. Each person sees their own. Before it expires the person and whoever recorded it
are told; the earlier one stays in the history.
