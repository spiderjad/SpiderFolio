## What it's for

IFS Food 8 and BRCGS 9 ask for up-to-date product specifications, a proven shelf life and labels reviewed
against that specification. In the Quality area, **Products** gathers what the company makes with its
technical sheet, its shelf life studies and its labels.

## The technical sheet

Whoever manages quality presses **New product** and fills in:

- the **code**, the **name** and the **family**;
- the **ingredients**, by weight as on the label;
- the **allergens** it contains and those it **may contain** (traces), among the 14 of Regulation
  1169/2011;
- the **shelf life** in days, the **storage** and whether it's **ready to eat** (it counts for the Listeria
  of Regulation 2073/2005);
- the **nutrition** per 100 g, if wanted (the eight values).

Each product has its **folder** in the Quality space (**Folder** button): the labels' artwork, the shelf life
reports and the sheet's document go there; the document is linked from **Edit sheet**.

The sheet has a **version**. Changing the ingredients, allergens, traces, nutrition or shelf life raises it:
the label approved before becomes **Outdated** until another one is approved.

## Approve a label

1. **Send to review**: choose the artwork from the folder and explain what changes. Whoever manages quality
   is told.
2. **Review**: the checklist of article 9 of Regulation 1169/2011 (name, ingredients, emphasised allergens,
   net quantity, date, storage, operator, lot, origin and nutrition), with the current sheet beside it.
   Origin and nutrition accept **Not applicable**.
3. **Approve** needs everything correct; **Reject**, the remarks. Approving a label leaves the previous one
   as **Superseded**.

Each product's label state is **Approved**, **Outdated**, **In review** or **No label**.

## Shelf life studies

**New study** records the start, the days to prove and whether it includes **Listeria monocytogenes**. When
it ends, **Record result**: **Passed** or **Not met**, the conclusion and the report. Not met can **open a
nonconformity** with origin **Laboratory**. A ready-to-eat product without a passed study shows as **No
shelf life study**.

## Who sees what

| Who                | What they see and do                                   |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Manage quality** | Everything: sheets, studies, sends and reviews labels. |
| **View quality**   | Reads the products, their labels and their studies.    |
