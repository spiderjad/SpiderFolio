## What it's for

A **required reading** asks a department or a person to read the current version of a [controlled document](/en/docs/controlled-documents) and confirm it with **I have read and understood**. That way you know who read each version, who didn't, and who doesn't even have access.

## Who can request it

Anyone whose role has the **Request reads** capability, and heads and deputies within their area. Readings can only be requested for documents with a published version.

## Request a reading

1. Open the document's **Document control** (on a page, from **More actions**; on a file, from its menu).
2. In the **Required reading** section, click **Request reading**.
3. Choose **Department** (and check **Include subareas** if subdepartments should read it too) or **Person**.
4. Optionally, set a **Due date (optional)**.
5. Click **Request reading**. Each person gets a notification ("You must read “…”").

If someone in scope doesn't have access to the document, SpiderFolio warns you before and after ("3 people don’t have access to this document"): they won't be able to read or confirm it until they do. If you manage the document's access, **Give them read access** gives them the **View** level with the same scope as the reading; otherwise, ask whoever manages it.

To remove a requested reading, use the remove button next to the department or person.

## Confirm a reading

If you were asked to read a document, you'll see it in [My documents](/en/docs/my-documents) › **To read**, in **My to-dos** on Home and in the document's own **Required reading** section ("You must read and confirm version 3.").

1. Open the document and read it.
2. Click **I have read and understood**.

Only you can confirm your reading: nobody does it for someone else. If the version changed since the one you read, you'll see the **Reason for the change** and **What changed since version …, which you read**, so you only read what's new.

On the shop floor, people who sign in with a username and PIN on a tablet go straight to the documents they need to read, and their session closes once they confirm.

## New versions

When a new version is published, whoever publishes it decides whether to request a new reading (it's checked by default):

- **With a new reading**: everyone who had to read the previous version must read the new one, due 7 days after publication.
- **Without a new reading** (minor change): previous readings remain valid.

## Reminders

- **Automatic**: at 8:00 in the company's time zone, people who haven't confirmed get a notification 3 days before the due date, on the due date and once a week after it's overdue. Seven days after it's overdue, the head of their department is notified too.
- **Manual**: in compliance, **Remind** notifies one person and **Remind everyone who hasn’t read it** notifies everyone pending. Each person gets at most one manual reminder per day.

## Compliance

The document's **Required reading** section shows the summary ("12 read · 3 pending · 1 without access · 0 overdue"), and **View compliance** opens the per-person detail, with each status (**Read**, **Pending**, **Overdue**, **No access**), a status filter and **Export CSV**. The export is recorded in the audit log.

To see all documents at once, go to **Administration** › **Controlled documents** › **Compliance**, by document or by department. Heads also see the **Reading compliance in your area** card on [Home](/en/docs/home).
