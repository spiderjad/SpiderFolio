## What it's for

The **planning of the preventive activity** gathers every measure of the company in a single list, as article
16 of Spain's Law 31/1995 asks: what has to be done, by whom, by when and why. Whoever manages the area sees it
in the **Planning** section.

Each measure has:

- a **responsible person** and a **due date**;
- a **priority**: high, medium or low;
- if you want, an **estimated cost**;
- its **origin**, with a link to what created it.

| Origin                   | Where it's created                                                                                        |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Risk assessment**      | In an EVR's controlled document window, **New preventive measure**.                                       |
| **Inspection**           | In a form record with **non-conforming** answers (the monthly inspection), **Create preventive measure**. |
| **Accident or incident** | On the case, **Add measure** (see [Accidents](/en/docs/accidents-and-rates#investigate-a-case)).          |
| **Drill**                | On the drill, **New measure** (see [Emergencies](/en/docs/emergencies-and-drills)).                       |
| **Committee**            | On the meeting, **New agreement** (see [Committee](/en/docs/safety-committee)).                           |
| **Other**                | In the planning, **New measure**.                                                                         |

## Follow the measures

- Filter by status (pending, overdue, done or all), origin, priority, responsible person and dates; the filters
  stay in the address, so you can bookmark or share a view.
- **Export CSV** downloads what's shown, for example for the yearly report or the audit.
- The responsible person is told 7 days before, the day before and on the day, and sees it in **My measures**,
  where they mark it done. Whoever manages the area can also edit it or mark it done from the planning.

## Accident measures

An accident's measures are health data. Whoever sees the planning without **Health data: accidents and fitness
for work** only reads “Accident or incident”, not which one, and can't change them.
