## What it's for

The **Emergencies** section (for whoever manages the area) brings together what's needed to be ready for an
emergency: the plan, who does what and proof that it's practised. It has two tabs: **Emergency team** and
**Drills**.

## The emergency plan

Turning the area on creates the controlled document type **PEM** (“Emergency plan”), with a yearly review. Make
your plan a [controlled document](/en/docs/controlled-documents) of that type and the screen links to it.

In a company that already had the area on, the type is created the first time someone who manages it opens
**Emergencies** or saves the settings.

## The emergency team

The team is made of **roles** with their people. It starts with Emergency chief, Intervention chief, First
response team, Second response team, First aid and Evacuation (in Latin America, a coordinator and brigades).
Rename them, archive them or add others with **New role**.

- **People**: add each person to their role; they can have a site or a shift.
- **Required training**: a role can require a [Training](/en/docs/training-and-fitness) course. Next to each
  person you see whether it's up to date, due soon, expired or missing, and a notice at the top counts who lacks
  it.
- **My emergency role**: everyone sees their role in the team on their own sheet.

## Record a drill

In **Drills**, click **Record a drill** and note:

- the date, the **scenario** (fire, evacuation, chemical spill, ammonia leak or another emergency) and the
  area;
- whether it was announced and how many people took part;
- the **evacuation time**;
- **what went wrong** and the observers.

The list filters by scenario and dates (the filters stay in the address).

## What comes out of a drill

From a drill, **New measure** creates a [planning](/en/docs/preventive-planning) measure linked to it (origin
“Drill”), with its owner and due date.

A year after the last drill, whoever manages the area gets the notice that another one is due (in
[Expiries](/en/docs/expiries), and also the department that owns the emergency plan).
