## What it's for

IFS Food 8 and BRCGS 9 ask to record and answer every complaint on time, to analyse their trends, to be ready
to act if a product has to be recalled and to test it at least once a year. The Quality area has
**Complaints** and **Crisis and recall**.

## Record a complaint

Anyone in the company can record the complaint they receive (for example, the salesperson who deals with the
customer): **Record complaint** asks for

- **who complains**: a **customer**, a **consumer** or the **authority**, and their name;
- for a consumer, their **phone or email**: it's personal data, and only whoever manages quality and the
  complaint's owner see it;
- the **product** and the **lot**, the **category** (foreign body, allergen, microbiological, quality,
  labelling or packaging) and what they complain about. Whoever views quality can pick the lot from
  [Traceability](/en/docs/traceability), to open its trace from the complaint;
- the date it was received.

It's **Open**, in the hands of whoever manages quality (or the owner chosen by whoever manages it), with its
deadline: the date received plus the company's **days to answer** (10 by default; changed in
**Administration** › **Security** › **General**, **Food quality** card). The owner is told 7 days before, the
day before and on the day; overdue, it shows in red.

## Answer and close

On the complaint's record, its owner or whoever manages quality:

- **Answer**: the answer, its date and whether the complaint was **justified**. It's **Answered**.
- **Open nonconformity**: creates the NC with origin **Complaint**, the chosen severity and the product and
  lot, to find the cause and the actions (see [Nonconformities and CAPA](/en/docs/nonconformities)). The
  complaint links it.
- **Close**: marks it finished.

## Indicators

In **Complaints** › **Indicators**: the year's complaints, the **justified** ones and the **complaints per
million units**, by month and in total, with the split by category. For the per million figure, whoever
manages quality enters each month's **units sold** in the same table; months without units don't count.

## Crisis and recall

**Crisis and recall** gathers what you need at hand if a product has to be recalled:

- **Crisis team**: the company's people with their role (coordination, quality and traceability,
  communication…).
- **Contacts**: the health authority, customers, the insurer, the laboratory and others, with their phone and
  email.
- **Recall tests**: each **drill** (or real recall) with the product, the lot, the traceability (backward,
  forward or both ways), the **minutes** it took to trace the lot, the **% recovered** and the result. A year
  after the last test, whoever recorded it is told another one is due. From a lot's trace, **Record as a
  recall test** opens the test with the lot, the direction and the minutes already filled in (see
  [Traceability](/en/docs/traceability)).
- **Communication templates**: the notes for the authority and customers are controlled documents in the
  Quality space; the button at the top opens it.

## Who sees what

| Who                 | What they see and do                                                          |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality**  | Everything: complaints, units sold, team, contacts and tests.                 |
| **View quality**    | Reads the complaints (without consumers' contact), the indicators and crisis. |
| A complaint's owner | Sees it with the contact, answers it, opens its NC and closes it.             |
| Everyone            | Records complaints and sees the ones they recorded.                           |
