## What it's for

A **nonconformity** (NC) is something that didn't meet what it should: a temperature out of limits, a wrong
label, an audit finding, a supplier without a certificate. **CAPA** (corrective and preventive actions) is
what's done to solve it and keep it from happening again. IFS Food 8 and BRCGS 9 ask for both with evidence:
what happened, why, what was done and that it worked.

Each NC has a consecutive code per year (**NC-2026-0001**) and goes through these steps:

1. **Open**: someone records it.
2. **Under analysis**: the root cause is looked for.
3. **With actions**: the actions are set, each with a responsible person and a date.
4. **Under verification**: with every action done, it's checked the problem didn't come back.
5. **Closed**: the check says it was effective.

It can be **cancelled** with a reason before closing; it's never deleted.

## Record a nonconformity

**Whoever manages quality**: in **Nonconformities**, **New nonconformity**. Besides the title, what happened,
when and where, choose:

- the **origin**: internal, CCP or record out of limits, internal or external audit, complaint, supplier or
  other;
- the **severity**: minor, major or critical (an IFS KO is entered as critical);
- the **owner**, who handles it, and if you like, the **department**.

**Anyone**: **Report a nonconformity**, under **Mine** in the Quality menu. It only asks what happened, when
and where. It's recorded as internal and major, in the company owner's hands until someone assigns it, and
whoever manages quality gets a notice in the bell. Whoever reported it sees it under **Reported by me**.

In both, if **product is affected** you note the lots and quantity, and the **immediate correction** is what
was done at the moment (set the product aside, stop the line).

## The record

A NC's record shows its steps at the top, with the current one highlighted, and below its details, the root
cause, the actions, the check, the evidence and the history.

<Shot
  name="calidad-nc"
  alt="An audit nonconformity with its steps, its details and the 5 whys analysis"
/>

- **Edit details** changes what was recorded; reassigning it to another owner is for whoever manages quality,
  and the new owner gets a notice.
- **Evidence** › **Open the folder** creates (the first time) and opens that NC's folder inside
  **Nonconformities**, in the Quality space, to upload photos, reports and records. Its files are seen by
  whoever has access to that folder, like any file.
- **History** gathers every step: who, when and what changed. It's also in the audit log.

## The root cause

In **Root cause**, choose the **method**:

- **5 whys**: ask why it happened, and why that happened, until you reach the source (up to 7 lines).
- **Ishikawa (6M)**: note the possible causes under people, method, machine, material, measurement and
  environment.
- **Other**: free text.

Write the **conclusion** and click **Save the analysis**. The NC moves to **Under analysis**. Look for why it
happened, not who: that way the action keeps it from happening again.

## The actions

In **Actions**, **Add action** asks for the type, what has to be done, the responsible person and the date:

- **Correction**: fixes what's happening now (replace the broken heater).
- **Corrective**: removes the cause so it doesn't happen again (add it to preventive maintenance).
- **Preventive**: keeps it from happening elsewhere (check similar equipment).

With the first action the NC moves to **With actions**. The responsible person is told 7 days before, the
day before and on the day, sees it in **My actions** and in **My to-dos**, and marks it done. The **Actions**
list gathers every NC's, filtered by status and type.

## The effectiveness check

When every action is done, the NC moves by itself to **Under verification** and the date is proposed: the
company's verification days after today (30 by default). On that day its owner gets a notice. The owner or
whoever manages quality can change the date.

<Shot
  name="calidad-verificacion"
  alt="The effectiveness check: the date, what was checked and the buttons to close or not"
/>

Whoever manages quality writes **what was checked** and chooses:

- **It was effective: close**: the NC is **Closed**, and whoever reported it gets a notice.
- **It wasn't effective**: it goes back to **With actions** to add new ones; the failed check stays on the
  record.

A closed NC can be **Reopened** with a reason: it goes back to **With actions**.

## From a form record

A record with **non-conforming** answers (a value outside its minimum or maximum, an answer marked as
non-conforming) can open a NC with origin **CCP or record out of limits**, linked to the record and with the
fields that failed:

- **By hand**: on the record, **Open nonconformity** (whoever manages quality or the form's person in
  charge). If the record already has an open one, the button takes you to it.
- **By itself**: in the form's settings, the **Quality** card › **Open a nonconformity when a record isn't
  conforming**, with the severity and the owner. Meant for control records: cold rooms, CCPs, metal
  detector.

It works alongside **Create preventive measure** from [Health & safety](/en/docs/preventive-planning): the
same record can have both.

## Export

**Export CSV** downloads the filtered nonconformities, with their code, status, owner, actions and root
cause, for the management review or the audit. Whoever sees them all can do it.
