Security in SpiderFolio has three parts:

- **The company's rules**, in **Administration › Security**: who sees whom, what heads can do and how
  documents are approved.
- **Your account's security**, in **My profile**: two-step verification and your open sessions.
- **The audit log**: who did what and when.

Requiring two-step verification for the whole company and restricting email domains are part of the
**Advanced security policies** plugin (see below).

## The company's rules

They're in **Administration › Security**, **General** tab, and they're **owner only**. (Admins with **Manage
security** only see the **Email** tab: see [SMTP email](/en/docs/smtp-email).) Each change saves instantly and
is recorded in the audit log.

<Shot name="seguridad" alt="Administration › Security, General tab, with the language, people visibility, heads and controlled document settings" />

### Company language

The **Default language** is used by everyone who hasn't chosen one in **My profile**. It's also used for
invitations and controlled document PDFs.

### People visibility

Sets who members see in the people directory (when mentioning and sharing). Under **Members see** you choose:

- **The whole company** (the default).
- **Only their department**: they see the people in their departments and the heads of the other areas. They
  can always share with a department by its name.

The owner, admins and heads always see the whole company. Readers and guests don't see the directory.

### Heads and deputies

**Heads can invite to their area** (on by default): lets heads and deputies invite people to their
departments, only as a member or reader. See [Invite people](/en/docs/invite-people).

### Controlled documents

**Allow self-approval** (off by default). When it's off, whoever submits a document for review can't approve
it. If you turn it on, each self-approval is recorded in the audit log and the history as "approved by the
author".

## Your two-step verification

Besides your password, signing in asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator,
Microsoft Authenticator or another). If someone gets your password, they still can't get in without your phone.

It's in **My profile**: click your name at the bottom of the side menu, then **My profile**. The section is
**Two-step verification**.

<Shot name="perfil" alt="My profile, with the Two-step verification section and the open sessions" />

### Turn it on

1. In **Two-step verification**, click **Turn on**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you can't scan it, click **Can't scan it? Show the key**
   and type it into the app.
3. Enter the 6-digit code shown in the app and click **Turn on**.
4. **Your recovery codes** appear: 10 codes for signing in if you lose your phone. Each one works only once.
   Click **Copy** or **Download**, keep them somewhere safe and click **I've saved them**. They're not shown
   again.

From then on the section says **On**, since when, and how many recovery codes you have left. We email you
every time it's turned on or off.

### Recovery codes

If you're running low, or think someone saw them, click **Generate new codes**. The old ones stop working
right away.

### Turn it off

Click **Turn off** and enter your **Password** and an **App code or recovery code**. Your account is then
protected by your password only, and your recovery codes and trusted devices stop working. If your company
requires two-step verification, you'll have to turn it on again next time you sign in.

People who sign in with a username and PIN (shop-floor tablets) don't use two-step verification. If you see
"Two-step verification isn't available yet," the installation is missing `MFA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`: let whoever runs
the server know.

### When you sign in

After your password, enter the **Code** from your app and click **Verify**. If you don't have your phone,
enter one of your recovery codes in the same box.

If you tick **Remember this device for 30 days**, that browser becomes a **trusted device** and won't ask for
the code for 30 days. Only use it on your own computer. Trusted devices are cleared when you turn verification
off or when an admin resets it.

### Actions that ask you to confirm it's you

For the most sensitive things we ask for the code even though you're already signed in, if you haven't
confirmed it in the last 10 minutes: generating new codes, turning verification off, changing your email,
changing the company policy and resetting someone else's verification. You'll see **Confirm it's you**: enter
the app code (or a recovery code) and click **Confirm**.

## If someone loses their phone

If a person lost their phone and their codes too, an admin can reset their verification:

1. In **Administration › Members**, open the menu on that person's row.
2. Click **Reset two-step verification** and confirm.

Their linked app, recovery codes and trusted devices are deleted, and their sessions are closed. The person
gets an email notice and sets it up again at their next sign-in.

To do this you need **Manage members** and **your own** two-step verification turned on (otherwise you'll see
"Turn on your own two-step verification to reset someone else's"). You can't reset your own, and an admin
can't reset another admin's or the owner's. It's recorded in the audit log.

## Open sessions

In **My profile › Open sessions** you see where you're signed in, with the IP, since when and when it was last
used. Yours says **This session**. Click **Sign out** on any you don't recognize.

- Changing your password closes your other sessions.
- An admin with **Suspend members** can **Sign out their sessions** for anyone from **Members**; they're
  closed in that account's other companies too.
- Suspending someone removes their access to the company right away.

## Require two-step verification for the whole company

This, and the allowed email domains, belong to the **Advanced security policies** plugin. It has to be
installed on the server and included in your license (see [License and plugins](/en/docs/license-and-plugins)).
With the plugin active, the owner gets a new section in **Administration**. The plugin's screens are currently
in Spanish only, so the Spanish labels are given in brackets below; the section is called **Políticas
avanzadas**.

### Mandatory two-step verification

Under **Require two-step verification** (**Exigir verificación en dos pasos**) you choose:

- **Don't require** (**No exigir**).
- **Owners and admins** (**A propietarios y administradores**): includes anyone with a role that manages
  members, security, company settings or roles.
- **Everyone except external guests** (**A todos (salvo invitados externos)**).

To require it, you must have it turned on yourself first (otherwise you'd lock yourself out) and confirm your
code. Tablet (PIN) accounts are never included.

Anyone who doesn't have it will see **Turn on two-step verification** with **Turn on now** when they sign in,
and can't continue until they set it up.

### Allowed email domains

Under **Domains** (**Dominios (uno por línea o separados por coma)**) enter your company's domains, for
example `company.com` (up to 20), one per line or separated by commas, and click **Save domains** (**Guardar
dominios**). From then on, only people with an email on those domains can be invited. It doesn't apply to
external guests, and people already in the company aren't affected. Leaving it empty allows any domain.

Single sign-on (SSO) with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace is coming later.

If the license expires, these policies stop applying and are kept for when you load it again; anyone who
already had verification turned on keeps using it.

## The audit log

In **Administration › Audit log** you see who did what and when in your company: sign-ins and failed attempts,
changes to members, roles and departments, security changes, verification resets, the email sender, limits,
the license and what happens to content. Events can't be changed.

<Shot name="auditoria" alt="The Audit log, with the filters at the top and the list of events" />

- You need **View the audit log**; to download it, **Export the audit log**.
- Filter by **Person**, **Area**, **Action**, **Item** and dates (**From**, **To**). **Clear filters** brings
  back the full list.
- Click an event to see the **Event details**: when, who, the action, the item, the **IP address**, the
  **Device** and the event data.
- **Export CSV** downloads the log with the applied filters. The export is recorded too.

How long it's kept depends on **Keep the audit log for** in [Limits and usage](/en/docs/limits): with no
limit, everything is kept.
