## What it's for

IFS Food 8 and BRCGS 9 ask for a risk-based testing plan: product, surfaces, environment (Listeria in the
ready-to-eat areas) and water, with their criteria and the follow-up of trends. In the Quality area,
**Sampling plan** gathers the sampling points and their results.

## Create a point

Whoever manages quality presses **New point** and chooses:

- the **type** (product, surfaces, environment or water), the **point** and the **zone**;
- the **product**, for a product sample;
- the **parameter** (for example, _Listeria monocytogenes_), the **criterion** and, for a value, the
  **maximum limit** and its **unit**;
- **every how many days**, the **next sample**, the **laboratory** and the **responsible** person, who is told
  7 days before, the day before and on the day.

Each point shows **Up to date**, **Due soon** or **Overdue**, like prerequisites.

## Record a result

The point's responsible person (or whoever manages quality) presses **Record result** and enters the sample
date and the **value** or the **presence** (detected or not detected). The screen proposes conforming or not
by the limit; the person confirms it. The laboratory report is chosen from the **Sampling plan** folder of the
Quality space (the folder button opens it to upload it).

Not conforming: **Open a nonconformity** creates it with origin **Laboratory**, the point and the parameter in
the title, in the hands of the point's responsible person (see
[Nonconformities and CAPA](/en/docs/nonconformities)).

## Trends

Each point's **history** shows its results with the limit. When the last two are not conforming, the point is
marked **Repeated**.

## Who sees what

| Who                          | What they see and do                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality**           | Everything: creates, edits, archives and records.             |
| **View quality**             | Reads every point and its history.                            |
| A point's responsible person | Sees it in **Mine** › **My samples** and records its results. |
