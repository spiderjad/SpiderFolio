## Sign in

1. Open your installation's address followed by `/login`.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**. Use the eye icon to show or hide the password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

If something doesn't match you'll see "Incorrect email or password." After several failed attempts in a row you'll have to wait a few minutes before trying again.

## Sign in with your company account

If your company signs people in with its directory (Active Directory or Microsoft Entra ID), it will give you
the address of its sign-in screen: your installation's address followed by `/login/` and the company's code
(for example, `/login/embutidos-demo`). If the installation has a single company, it also shows on `/login`.

- **With the directory's username and password:** type your company **Username** and **Password** (the
  same as on your work computer) and click the company's button.
- **With Microsoft:** click the Microsoft button, pick your work account and you're back in the app,
  signed in.

The first time, your SpiderFolio account is created with the directory's details. If you have two-step
verification on, it asks for your code just like with your email (unless your company accepts Microsoft's).
If you already had an account with the same email, it isn't linked on its own: ask your company's owner to
link it.

Some companies only let people in through the directory. If you sign in with your email and password,
you'll see **Sign in with your work account**: sign out and sign in again with the company's button. Owners
can always sign in with their email and password.

## Language

The sign-in, sign-up, password reset and invitation screens are shown in your browser's language (English or Spanish). To change it, use the **ES / EN** switch at the top right. Once you sign in, the app switches to your account's language, which you can change in **My profile**.

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot your password?**.
2. Enter your email and click **Send link**.
3. Open the email you receive. The link expires in 1 hour.
4. Enter your **New password** and click **Save password**.
5. Sign in with the new one.

For security, the screen gives the same answer whether or not an account exists with that email. If the link has expired, click **Request a new link**.

## Sign out

Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the side menu and confirm. You'll be signed out of the app on that device; if you have several tabs open, they are all signed out.
