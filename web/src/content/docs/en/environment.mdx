## What it's for

The **Environment** module brings together what an environmental inspection or an audit asks to see: the
company's **permits** and their expiries, what has to be done **every few months** (emission checks,
discharge analyses, declarations) and the **waste and animal by-products record** (see
[Waste and animal by-products](/en/docs/waste-and-animal-by-products)). It's designed first for the food and
meat industry in Spain.

## Turning it on

In **Administration › Security › General**, the **Environment** card. Only the **owner**. Turning it on adds
the **Environment** area to the rail and creates its **documents space**, where each permit and each waste
manager has its folder.

Turning it off hides the area and pauses the notices. **It deletes nothing.**

## Who does what

Three capabilities, in **Administration › Roles and permissions**:

- **Manage environment**: the whole module. By default, owner and admins.
- **View environment**: reads everything without changing anything (management, preparing an inspection).
- **Record waste collections**: records collections, without touching the catalog, the waste managers or the
  permits. Meant for maintenance or dispatch; it sees the collections it records.

## Permits

In **Environment › Permits**, **New permit** asks for:

- The **type**: integrated environmental permit, environmental license or notice, discharge permit, waste
  producer registration, animal by-products registration, air emissions or other.
- The **name**, the **authority** and the **file or registration number**.
- When it was issued, when it **expires** and, if its conditions are reviewed earlier, the **review** date.
- The **main conditions** and an **owner**.

Each permit has its folder in the module's space (the **Documents** link) to keep the decision, the annexes
and the correspondence. The list marks in color the ones expiring soon or already expired.

## Recurring obligations

What has to be done every few months: “Boiler emissions check” every 36 months, “Discharge analysis” every 3,
“Annual packaging declaration” every 12. Each obligation has its **period**, the **next date**, an owner and,
if it comes from a permit, which one.

When it's done, **Done** asks for the date and a note (for example, the company that did it or the report's
number) and works out the next one. The history stays in the obligation.

## Notices

Permits, obligations, waste managers' authorizations and each waste's maximum storage time give notice before
they're due: to their owner or, without one, to whoever manages the module. They also show in **Expiries**
and in the recipient's **My tasks**.

Archiving a permit or obligation takes it out of the list and the notices without deleting it; it can be
restored.
