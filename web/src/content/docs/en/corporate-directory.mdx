The **Corporate directory** connects SpiderFolio with your company's directory:

- People sign in with their **Active Directory** account (the Windows one) or their **Microsoft 365** one.
- The first time, their account is created. Their **groups decide the role and departments**.
- **Every hour** it syncs: whoever is no longer in the directory, was disabled or left the allowed groups
  is suspended; whoever changed groups changes role or departments. Owners are never suspended.
- It includes **Sign in with Microsoft**: if you already used it, it keeps working the same, with the same
  setup and the same linked people.

It's a **paid** plugin. You need to be the company's **owner**, with two-step verification on.

## 1. Install the plugin

1. In **Administration › License and plugins**, copy the **Company ID** and send it to us
   ([contact](mailto:contacto@spiderjad.com)).
2. We send you a `.sfplugin` file signed for your company. Upload it in **Upload plugin**.
3. Tap **Restart now**. A minute later **Corporate directory** shows in the Administration menu.

## 2. Active Directory with the assistant

No LDAP knowledge needed. First, ask your IT person for a **service account**: a normal Active Directory
user (not an administrator), for example `svc-spiderfolio`, with a password that doesn't expire.
SpiderFolio only uses it to read the directory.

<Shot name="plugin-asistente" alt="The Active Directory assistant showing the server's certificate and the question Is this your server?" />

1. **Domain**: type your network's domain (for example, `company.local`) and tap **Find the server**.
   SpiderFolio looks it up through DNS. If it doesn't find it, type the domain controller's name (for
   example, `dc01.company.local`).
2. **Server**: SpiderFolio downloads the server's certificate and shows it to you. If the name is your
   server's, tap **Yes, it's my server**: from then on it only connects to that one.
3. **Service account**: type `svc-spiderfolio@company.local` and its password. Tap **Test**: it has to say
   how many people the directory has.
4. **Groups**: tick the groups whose people may sign in (with none ticked, anyone may). Below, in **What
   does each group give?**, pick a group and the role and departments its people get. If someone is in
   several, the first one with a role wins: reorder them with the arrows.
5. **Review**: the button text (optional), whether it shows on the sign-in screen and whether it syncs
   every hour. Tap **Save**.

People sign in with their Windows username (for example, `agarcia`) and their usual password.

**Advanced settings**: for people who know LDAP, the same content field by field, plus StartTLS, another
search base or another username attribute.

## 3. Microsoft 365

On the same screen, **Connection with Microsoft 365** is the one in
[Sign in with Microsoft](/en/docs/sign-in-with-microsoft) (follow its steps 2 and 3). The only difference:
in Azure, besides `User.Read.All`, grant `GroupMember.Read.All` so SpiderFolio can read the groups.

Below, in **Microsoft 365 groups**, say which groups may sign in and what each one gives. Groups are
written by their **Object ID** (in Azure: **Microsoft Entra ID › Groups › the group**).

## 4. Sync now

<Shot name="plugin-directorio" alt="The Corporate directory screen with the Sync now button at the top and the Active Directory summary" />

At the very top there's **Sync now**. It first shows what would change:

- who is **suspended** and why (no longer in the directory, account disabled or outside the groups);
- who is **reactivated**;
- who gets a **new role, departments or name**.

Check the list and tap **Apply these changes**. New people don't show: their account is created the first
time they sign in.

**The brake**: if a sync would suspend more people than the share set (10 % by default), it isn't applied
on its own and you get a bell notice. That way a setup mistake doesn't lock everyone out. Check the
preview and, if it's right, tap **Apply anyway**.

## 5. People who were already here

If someone already had a SpiderFolio account, link it in **Link someone who was already here** with their
directory username. Otherwise, signing in tells them their email is already in use.

**Directory only**: once everything works, you can make anyone who isn't an owner sign in only with their
directory account. Owners keep their email and password.

## If the license expires

Active Directory stops working and the sync stops (you get a notice). If **Sign in with Microsoft** (free)
is installed, people keep signing in with Microsoft, with the same setup and linked as before.

## Common problems

- **«The server doesn't accept encrypted connections (LDAPS, port 636)»**: your Active Directory doesn't
  have LDAPS turned on. Give your IT person the [short guide to turn on LDAPS](/en/docs/enable-ldaps).
- **«That server wasn't found»**: check the name, or that the SpiderFolio server uses your network's DNS.
  Try the full name (`dc01.company.local`).
- **«The server didn't answer»**: a firewall blocks port 636 between the SpiderFolio server and the domain
  controller.
- **«The certificate isn't for that name»**: type the server's name as it shows in **Names**.
- **«The server rejected the service account»**: username or password mistyped, account disabled or
  password expired.
- **«The server's certificate isn't the one you confirmed»**: the server changed its certificate (it
  happens when it's renewed). Open the assistant again and confirm the new one.
- **Someone can't sign in**: check they're in one of the allowed groups and their account isn't disabled.
  If the sync suspended them, they show in the preview with the reason.
