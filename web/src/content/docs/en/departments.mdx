## What they're for

The **role** sets what a person can administer; the **department**, what content they see. That's why access
to spaces and documents is given to departments rather than person by person: when someone changes area,
their access changes with them.

Departments form a tree: **Finance** can have the sub-areas **Accounting** and **Treasury**, and each
sub-area its own, with no limit on levels.

## Where to find it

- **Owner and admins** (**Manage departments** capability): in **Administration › Departments**, with every
  department.
- **Heads and deputies**: in **Administration › Departments**, only with the ones they lead and their
  sub-areas. Outside their scope, a department doesn't exist for them.
- **Members with a full seat**: in the main menu, next to **Files**, to look at the structure without
  changing anything. Readers and guests don't see it.

## Two views

Next to the title you choose the **View**:

- **Areas**: the tree on the left and the selected department on the right. On a phone, the tree goes on
  top and the details below.
- **Org chart**: the whole company as a tree, with a search box. Clicking a department takes you back to
  **Areas** with that department open.

## The tree

- Click the **name** to open that department. The **arrow** only expands or collapses the branch.
- Each row shows how many people it has. At the top there's a search box and, if you can manage
  departments, **New department**.
- With the keyboard: ↑ ↓ move, → expands, ← collapses or goes up to the parent, Enter selects.
- The selected department is kept in the page address: Back, Forward and direct links work.

## The details

At the top you see the path (for example **Finance › Accounting**, each level is a link), the name, the
**Head** (★) and the **Deputies** (☆). If it's an area you lead, you'll see the **Your area** label. There
are two tabs:

- **People**: who belongs to it. Each row has the position selector (**Head**, **Deputy**, **Member**), which
  saves instantly, and a menu with **Remove from department**. **Add people** searches the company directory.
- **Sub-areas**: the direct sub-areas, with their head and number of people. Click one to open it. With
  **Manage departments** you also get **New sub-area**.

## Heads and deputies

Each department has at most one **head** and can have several **deputies**. The position isn't the role: a
member can be the head of their area, and an admin can be a head too (that only organizes the org chart,
since they already manage the whole company).

The position gives scope over the department **and all its sub-areas**, at any depth. If someone stops being
head or deputy, they lose that scope right away.

What they can do, always within their area:

| | Head | Deputy |
|---|---|---|
| See their people in **My team** (email, seat, status, last seen) | Yes | Yes |
| Add, remove and move people between their areas | Yes | Yes |
| Appoint or remove deputies | Yes | No |
| Invite people to their area as a member or reader | Yes, if the owner allows it | Yes, if the owner allows it |
| Approve their area's controlled documents | Yes | Yes |
| Manage their department's spaces | Yes | Yes |

What they can't do: change anyone's role, seat or status, appoint heads, or act on admins, the owner, other
heads or themselves.

To act in the position they need a **full seat**. A reader can be listed as head, but the position gives them
no scope; the **Structure review** (in **Members**, **Org chart** view) flags it.

Appointing a new head makes the previous one a member of the department. Only the owner and admins appoint
heads.

## Create, edit and delete

You need **Manage departments**.

1. Click **New department** (or **New sub-area** from the **Sub-areas** tab).
2. Enter the **Name** (unique in the company) and, if you like, the **Description** and **Color**.
3. Under **Inside**, choose the parent department, or **None (top level)**.
4. Choose the **Head**, if you already know who it is.
5. Click **Create**.

The menu in the details header (the three dots) has **Edit** (also to move it inside another department),
**Create its space** and **Delete**. Before deleting we tell you how many people will no longer belong to it
and lose the access it gave them; its sub-departments move up one level. It can't be undone.

Every change to departments, membership and positions is recorded in the audit log.
