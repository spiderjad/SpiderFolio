## What they're for

Many documents are valid until a date: a certificate, an insurance policy, a supplier contract, a machine's inspection. An **expiry** gives a file or a page that date, with someone **responsible** who gets notices before it expires.

It's not the same as a [controlled document](/en/docs/controlled-documents)'s review date: a controlled document can have both.

## Set an expiry

In the explorer, open the **⋯** menu of a file, a page or a form and choose **Expiry**. It's also on the item's screen: on a page, **More actions** › **Expiry**; on a form, the **Expiry** button; in a file's preview, the **Expiry** button at the bottom.

<Shot name="vencimiento" alt="The Expiry dialog with the date, the responsible, the notice days and the note" />

1. Choose the date in **Expires on**.
2. In **Responsible**, choose a person or a department. With a department, the notices go to its **heads and deputies**. If you choose no one, you're responsible: someone always gets the notices.
3. In **Notify**, mark when to notify: by default **30 days before**, **7 days before** and **On the day**.
4. If needed, write a **Note** with what needs doing.
5. Press **Save**.

### Suggest with Bian

If [Bian](/en/docs/bian) is on, the dialog has the **Suggest with Bian** button: it reads the page's or the file's text and, if the document says until when it's valid ("valid until", "expires", "expiry date"…), it suggests the date. It can also suggest a note and someone responsible, if the document names them and they match a person or a department of the company. Under the button you see which sentence of the document the date comes from.

**It isn't saved on its own**: review the suggestion and press **Save**. It uses one Bian query from your quota, and only someone who can set the expiry can ask for it. Forms have no text to read.

If the responsible person can't see the item, SpiderFolio tells you when you save: they'll get the notices but won't be able to open it. Share it with them or choose someone else.

To remove it, open the same dialog and press **Remove the expiry**.

## Who can

- **See it**: whoever can see the item.
- **Set, change or remove it**: whoever can **edit** the item, or whoever has the **Manage expiry dates** capability in their role and can see it. Everyone else sees the expiry read-only.

Setting, removing and exporting expiries is recorded in the audit log.

## The badges

Every item with an expiry shows a badge with the time left, in its state's color:

- green, **Due in 31 days**, while it's valid;
- amber, **Due in 5 days**, when it's close (within the largest notice);
- red, **Expired 2 days ago**, once the date has passed.

They appear in the explorer, next to the name, and on the item's screen. Hover to see the date; press the badge to open the expiry.

## The notices

At **8:00 company time**, whoever is responsible gets a notice in the bell and by email on each notice day you marked. If you change the date, the notices start over.

An item in the **trash** doesn't notify; restore it and it notifies again. Deleted for good, its expiry goes too.

## When you upload a new version

When you upload a new version of a file with an expiry (with **Upload a new version** or by replacing the file), SpiderFolio offers to update it with **the same term it had**: if it expired a year after it was set, it proposes a year from today. You can **Update**, **Choose another day** or **Leave it**.

## The Expiries screen

In the side menu, **Expiries** shows all the ones you can see. The number next to it counts **only yours** (as the responsible, or of a department you lead) due soon or expired: **red** if any has expired, **amber** if they're only due soon.

<Shot name="vencimientos" alt="The expiries list with filters by space, department, responsible and state" />

- Filter by **Space**, **Department** (the responsible's or the space's), **Responsible**, **State** (**Valid**, **Due soon**, **Expired**) and dates.
- **Calendar** shows the month, with each item on its day in its state's color.
- **Export CSV** downloads the list with the same filters.

<Shot name="vencimientos-calendario" alt="The month calendar view with the expiries on their days" />

On **Home**, the **Your expiries** card shows yours due soon or expired.
