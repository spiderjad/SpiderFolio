Everyone joins the company through an invitation: they get an email with a link, create their account (or
use the one they already have) and end up with the role, seat and departments you chose.

## Before you start

- You need the **Invite people** capability. Heads and deputies can also invite to their area (see below).
- Your email must be confirmed. If it isn't, when you send you'll see "Confirm your email to be able to
  invite."
- The installation needs a working email server: see [SMTP email](/en/docs/smtp-email).

## Invite

1. In **Administration › Members**, click **Invite people**.
2. Enter the **Emails**: one per line, or separated by commas or spaces. Up to 50 at a time.
3. Choose the **Role**. Only the roles you can give appear.
4. The **Seat** stays on **Automatic**: a full seat for members and admins, reader for external guests. You
   can choose another one when the role allows it.
5. Tick the **Departments** they join. An external guest can't join departments.
6. Click **Send invitations**.

<Shot name="miembros" alt="The Members screen, with the Invite people and Import CSV buttons at the top right" />

Each person gets an email with a link that expires in **7 days**. Anyone who's already a member or already
has a pending invitation is skipped, and we tell you how many were skipped.

If your company uses the **Advanced security policies** plugin with allowed domains, you can only invite
emails from those domains (external guests are the exception). Otherwise you'll see "These emails aren't
from an allowed domain."

## Invite as a head or deputy

If you lead a department, you can invite to your area from **My team**, with these conditions:

- Only as a member or reader, never with an admin role.
- Only to your departments and their sub-areas.
- Each invitation shows up in **Pending invitations** and in the audit log, and admins can revoke it.

The owner can turn this off in **Administration › Security**, **Heads can invite to their area**.

## Pending invitations

Below the members table are the **Pending invitations**, with **Email**, **Role**, **Seat**, **Expires** and
**Invited by**. You can search by email and filter by **Status** (**All**, **Active**, **Expired**). On each
one:

- **Resend**: sends a new email and renews the deadline. The previous link stops working.
- **Revoke**: the link stops working and the seat is freed. You can invite the person again anytime.

A pending invitation takes up a seat (full or reader) until it's accepted, expires or is revoked.

## Import from a CSV

For many people, click **Import CSV**. The file has one row per person and these columns:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `email` | Required. |
| `role` | The name of a role. If missing, **Member**. |
| `departments` | Names separated by `\|`, for example `Quality\|Production`. |
| `seat` | `FULL` or `READER`. If missing, the one that matches the role. |

The first row holds the column names (the Spanish ones, `rol`, `departamentos` and `plaza`, work too).
Cells can be separated by commas or semicolons, so a file saved from Excel works as is.

1. Choose the **CSV file** (up to 200 KB and 500 rows) or **Or paste the content**.
2. Click **Preview**. Each row is marked **Ready**, **Skipped** (already a member or already invited) or
   **Error** with its reason: invalid email, duplicate in the file, a role or department that doesn't
   exist, a role you can't give, an invalid seat or a domain that isn't allowed.
3. When it looks right, click **Invite valid rows**.

## What the invited person sees

The link in the email opens the **You're invited to join** screen, with the company name, who invited them
and with which role:

- **If they don't have an account**, they enter their **Full name**, choose a **Password** (at least 10
  characters) and click **Accept and continue**. Their email is confirmed and their account takes the
  screen's language (their browser's, or the one they pick with the **ES / EN** switch).
- **If they already have an account**, they click **Sign in**, sign in with that account and then click
  **Accept invitation**. If they're signed in with a different account, the screen tells them.

If the link has expired or was already used, the screen says so: they need to ask whoever invited them for
a new one.
