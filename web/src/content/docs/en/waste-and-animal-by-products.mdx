## What it's for

Spain's **Ley 7/2022** requires a **chronological record** of waste (article 64) and **Regulation (EU)
142/2011** a register of the dispatches of **animal by-products**. In **Environment › Waste**, each collection
you record feeds both, and they're exported as PDF or CSV.

## The catalog

In the **Catalog** tab, each waste has:

- Its 6-digit **LER code**. With “\*” at the end it's **hazardous** (for example, 130205\* for used oil).
- If it's an animal by-product, its **category** (1, 2 or 3).
- The **treatment operation** (R for recovery, D for disposal: R3, R13, D10…).
- The usual **waste manager** and **carrier**, and the **treatment contract**.
- The **maximum storage time**: by default, the law's (6 months for hazardous; 24 for recovery and 12 for
  disposal otherwise). The app gives notice before a waste reaches it without being collected.

**Load the meat industry's waste** adds the usual ones (category 1, 2 and 3 by-products, treatment sludge,
oil, contaminated packaging, plastics, cardboard, fluorescent tubes) to adjust them. Existing ones with the
same name aren't touched.

## Waste managers and carriers

In the **Waste managers** tab, each company with its **tax ID**, its **NIMA**, its **authorizations** and when
they expire, and whether it's a **waste manager**, a **carrier** or both. Each has its documents folder
(authorization, treatment contract). The app gives notice before its authorization expires.

## Recording a collection

In the **Collections** tab, **Record collection** asks for the waste, the date, the **quantity** and its unit,
the waste manager and the carrier (by default, the waste's), the number of the **identification document
(DI)** or of the animal by-products **commercial document**, and the vehicle registration.

When it's recorded, the waste's storage counts again from that day. Whoever has **Record waste collections**
only sees the ones they recorded; whoever manages the module can **delete** a collection to correct a mistake
(it stays in the audit log).

## Exporting for the inspection

Choose the period with **From** and **To** and open **Export**:

- **Chronological record**: date, waste, LER code, whether it's hazardous, by-product category, quantity, waste
  manager and its NIMA, operation, carrier, document, vehicle and notes.
- **By-products register**: only animal by-products, with category, consignee and commercial document.
- **Annual summary**: the total by waste, waste manager and unit, for the declarations and the report.

Each export, as PDF or CSV, goes to the audit log.
