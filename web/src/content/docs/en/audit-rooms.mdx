## What they're for

In an audit (ISO, a customer's, a health inspection), the auditor asks to see certain documents and records. An **audit room** gives them access to that and nothing else: **no account nor seat**, **read-only**, until a date, and everything they see is a PDF with the watermark “Audit copy · [their email] · [date]”. Every time they come in, view or download something, it's recorded.

It's free, part of SpiderFolio. Whoever has the **Share audits** capability (`audit.share`) in their role builds it; see [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions).

## Create a room

In **Administration › Audit rooms**, press **New room**:

1. **Name**: for example, “ISO 22000 audit · November”.
2. **Auditor's email**: the link and the code to get in go there.
3. **Expires on**: up to 30 days. At the end of that day the room closes on its own.
4. **Allow downloads**: without it, the auditor sees the PDFs on screen, with no download button.

Once it's created, the auditor gets the link by email. Then you add what you share.

## What you share

- **Documents, folders and spaces**: from the explorer's **⋯** menu (or the space card's), **Add to an audit room**. A folder or a space includes what's inside it when it's viewed. Controlled documents are shown in their current version.
- **A controlled document's reports**: from its **⋯** menu, the **reading compliance** or the **approvals and versions history**.
- **A form's records**, between two dates: from the form's **⋯** menu.
- In the room, **Add a report**: a course's **training records** and the **PPE handouts** between two dates (with [Occupational health and safety](/en/docs/occupational-health-and-safety)), and the **audit log** filtered by dates and action.

Accidents, incidents and fitness are never shared: they're health data. You can only add what you can see, and each report needs the capability already needed to see it (for example, the audit log needs **View the audit log**). The room always shows what its creator sees: if they lose access to something, the auditor stops seeing it.

Everything is shown as a **watermarked PDF**: pages, PDFs, Word, Excel or PowerPoint files (converted) and images. Other formats can't be shown. It needs [Gotenberg](/en/docs/gotenberg).

## How the auditor gets in

1. They open the link in the email.
2. They press **Send me the code**: it goes to that same email. It works **only once**, for 10 minutes, and locks after 5 wrong tries.
3. They type the code and get in: they see only the room, with **View** (and **Download**, if you allowed it) on each item.

Their access lasts up to 8 hours; then they ask for the code again. They see nothing else of the company and take no seat.

## Accesses, resending and revoking

In the room, **The auditor's accesses** shows every entry, every document they viewed and every download, with date and IP. The first time they come in, whoever created the room gets a notice in the bell.

- **Send the link again**: if the auditor can't find the email.
- **Revoke the room**: they can't get in any more, right away, and it can't be reopened. What they already saw stays in the log.

Creating, revoking, adding and removing are also recorded in the company's audit log. Each person sees the rooms they created; the owner sees all of them.

Whatever is seen in a browser can always be captured: the watermark with the email and the date is what identifies each copy.
