## What it's for

**Home** is the first screen you see in your company. It greets you ("Hi, Ana. Here's what's happening at …") and brings together what you need to do and what's going on around you. Everything comes from real data, filtered by what you can see.

<Shot name="inicio" alt="Home with the company's key figures, activity and to-dos" eager />

While there's no content yet, each card shows zero and explains what will appear and what to do to get started.

## What the whole team sees

- **My to-dos**: the approvals and readings assigned to you, with their due date (**Due …** or **Overdue**) and the action one click away: **Review** or **Read**. If you have many, a link takes you to the full list. See [Approvals](/en/docs/approvals) and [Required reading](/en/docs/required-reading).
- **Ask Bian**: type a question and click **Ask** to find the answer in the documents you can already see. See [Bian](/en/docs/bian).
- **Recently shared with me**: the latest items shared with you, and who shared them. **See everything shared** opens **Shared**.
- **Favorites**: the spaces you starred.
- **Recent**: the last documents you opened.

## If you lead a department

Heads and deputies also see three cards about their area:

- **Reading compliance in your area**: per document, how many people read it, how many don't have access and how many readings are overdue. **Remind those pending** notifies them again (once a day).
- **Upcoming reviews (60 days)**: documents in your area that are due for review soon.
- **Waiting for approval**: what your area sent for review that hasn't been decided yet.

## If you're the owner or an admin

The company's key figures appear at the top:

- **Current documents**: controlled documents with their approved version in use.
- **In review**: waiting for the approver's decision.
- **Drafts**: not yet sent for review.
- **Reading compliance**: of the readings requested, the share already confirmed.
- **Active people (30 days)**: with some activity in the last month.
- **Storage used**: how much your company uses and, if there's a limit, how much is left (see [Limits and usage](/en/docs/limits)).

Below them are the **Activity — last 30 days** chart (uploads, publications and confirmed readings per day) and **Most viewed this month**, the documents opened by the most people. Content in confidential spaces you can't enter doesn't count.

## If you have a reader seat

People with a reader seat don't see Home: they go straight to [My documents](/en/docs/my-documents), with their readings and what's new in their area.

## Confirming your email

If you haven't confirmed your email yet, you'll see a notice with the **Resend email** button. Until you confirm it, you can't invite your team or publish documents.

## The side menu and the top bar

- The side menu has **Home**, **My documents**, **Files** and **Shared**; **Approvals** if you can approve documents; and the **Management** group with whatever you can administer. Anything you can't use doesn't appear.
- The **Create** menu offers **New page**, **Upload files** and **New space**, depending on your permissions.
- At the top you'll find **Search** (also Ctrl+K, see [Search](/en/docs/search)), **Ask Bian** and **Notifications** (the bell), with items shared with you, requested readings and approvals.
- On a phone, open the menu with the menu button at the top left. On large screens you can collapse it to show only the icons.
