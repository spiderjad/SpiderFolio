## What it's for

The **occupational health and safety** module (PRL in Spain, SST in Latin America) brings together what
prevention on the shop floor needs: handing out personal protective equipment signed by whoever receives it,
replacing it, and what each person lacks for their position. It builds on
[positions](/en/docs/departments#positions), controlled documents and forms.

For now it covers **PPE** handouts, **accidents and incidents**, **training** and **risk assessments**.
And each person's **fitness for work**. If you turn it on, also the coordination with
[contractors (CAE)](#contractors-cae).

## Turn it on

The owner turns it on in **Administration** › **Security** › **General**, under **Occupational health and
safety** ([Security and two-step verification](/en/docs/security-and-2fa)). There they also choose the
**Terms**: **Spain: PRL and EPI** or **Latin America: SST and EPP**. In English the app always says PPE.

With the module on, **Health & safety** shows in the side menu. Turning it off hides it, with its notices,
without deleting anything.

## How it's organized

Whoever manages the module opens **Health & safety** on the **Summary**: cards with what needs attention
(people missing PPE, PPE to replace, training expired or due soon and, with **Health data**, fitness due, open
measures and open cases), each with **See the list**. Their tabs are **Summary**, **People**, **Handouts**,
**Catalog**, **Training** and **Accidents**. Heads see **People**, **Handouts** and **Training** for their
area.

In the side menu, **Health & safety** shows a number with what's pending: for whoever manages it, the
Summary's total; for everyone else, the PPE they lack, their pending training and their measures.

<Shot name="resumen" alt="The Health & safety Summary with the cards of what needs attention" />

Whoever doesn't manage it goes straight to their own sheet. If they lack PPE or have training pending, they
also see it on [Home](/en/docs/home).

## Who sees what

| Who                                            | What they see                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| With **Manage occupational health and safety** | Every handout and the catalog; records and voids handouts.        |
| Heads and deputies                             | The handouts and PPE of the people in their area (and sub-areas). |
| Everyone else                                  | Their own handouts and PPE.                                       |

The capability is given in [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions); the Admin role already
has it.

## The catalog

Under **Handouts** › **Catalog** are your company's items. Click **New item** and fill in:

- **Name** (“Cut-resistant gloves”) and, if you like, a **Category** (“Hands”).
- **Handed out by size**: the size is asked for at each handout.
- **Life in months**: it's replaced N months after each handout, with notices (see below). Without a life,
  there's no replacement.
- **Positions that need it**: whoever has one of those positions and hasn't received it shows it under
  **Missing**.

An item no longer used is **Archived**: it's no longer offered at handouts and the earlier ones are kept.
**Show archived** lists them, and from each one you can **Restore** it.

<Shot name="epis-catalogo" alt="The PPE catalog, with each item's life and positions" />

## Record a handout

1. In **Handouts**, click **New handout**.
2. Under **Received by**, choose the person. If something their position requires is missing, it shows at
   the top (“Missing: …”).
3. Add the **Items** with their **Quantity** and, if they go by size, the **Size**.
4. The person reads the statement and **signs in the box** with a finger (on a tablet or phone) or the mouse.
5. Click **Record handout**.

The handout keeps who received it, who handed it out, the company's date and time, and the signature. It's
never changed or deleted.

## View a handout and its receipt

Opening a handout shows the items, the size, the **Replace on** day and the signature. **PDF receipt**
downloads the signed receipt to file or print; it needs [Gotenberg](/en/docs/gotenberg). Downloading it is
recorded in the audit log.

If it was entered wrongly (another person, another item), whoever manages the module can **Void** it with a
**Reason**. It still shows, marked as voided, and no longer counts for replacement.

## Each person's sheet

Each person has their **health and safety sheet**. It opens:

- from the **People** tab in Health & safety (whoever manages the module sees everyone; heads, their area),
  with a search box, **Position** and **Department** filters and an **Alerts** column: PPE missing, courses
  expired or pending and fitness due soon;
- from **Administration** › **Members**, in each person's menu: **Health and safety sheet**;
- your own, from **My profile** (whoever doesn't manage the module goes straight to it from Health &
  safety).

On someone else's sheet there are only actions on them: **Hand out PPE to…** and, with **Health data**,
**Record fitness**. **My measures** and **Report an incident** are on your own sheet and the Summary.

The sheet shows:

- **Missing**: what their position requires and they don't have, or have expired.
- **PPE they have**: the latest of each item, with its replacement day and its state (**Valid**, **Due
  soon** or **Expired**).
- **Handouts**: every one they received.

Each person sees their own by opening **Health & safety** in the menu.

**Download the sheet (PDF)** puts the sheet in a PDF: their PPE, what's missing and their training. For whoever
has **Health data: accidents and fitness for work** it also has their fitness and their accidents and
incidents, with a note that it's confidential; each person can download their own (with their fitness). It
needs [Gotenberg](/en/docs/gotenberg) and it's recorded in the audit log.

## Replacement notices

When an item with a life gets close to its replacement day, the person and whoever handed it out get a notice
in the bell and by email: 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day, at 8:00 in the company's time zone. A
new handout of the same item to the same person replaces the earlier one, and voiding a handout removes its
notice.

## Accidents and incidents

Anyone can **report** an accident, an incident or a near miss with **Report an incident** (on their sheet
or the Summary) or by scanning the form's QR code. It opens inside Health & safety, with today's date and the
time now already filled in, and once sent it goes back to where you were. They fill in the **Accident or incident** form, which the module
creates in its space: date and time, place, person affected, type, severity, what happened, causes, photos and
witnesses. Don't write medical data or diagnoses.

That form's fixed fields can't be removed nor change their type or options (in the editor they show **Fixed
field**); their text can change and you can add your own fields.

It's **health data**: cases are only seen by whoever has **Health data: accidents and fitness for work** (by
default, only the owner; see [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions)). Whoever reported a case
sees what they reported. Each new case gives a notice to whoever has that capability, in the bell and by email;
the email doesn't say what happened nor to whom, only who reported it and the link to the case.

Under **Accidents** you filter them by dates, type and status, and export them to CSV. Opening a case:

- **See what was reported** opens the form's record.
- **Add measure**: a corrective measure with a **Responsible** and a date (**By**). The responsible gets a
  notice 7 days before, the day before and on the day, and sees it in **My measures**, where they mark it done
  (without seeing the case's details).
- **Move to investigation** while the causes are looked into: the case is **Under investigation** (still among
  the open ones, and it can be filtered on its own); **Back to open** undoes it.
- **Close the case** once every measure is done; it can be **Reopened**.

## Training

A **course** is a [controlled document](/en/docs/controlled-documents) (the saw procedure, the emergency
plan): in **Training**, whoever manages the module clicks **New course**, chooses the document and, if they
like, its **Valid for months**.

- Who has to do it comes from its [required reading](/en/docs/required-reading): departments, **positions**
  or people.
- The **“I have read and understood”** of the current version is the **record**, with its date. With a
  validity, it expires: the person is told 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day, and the course shows
  **Confirm I did it again**.
- **Record in-person training**: whoever manages the module chooses the **Attendees** and the **Date**, and
  each attendee signs in their box with a finger (for example, passing the tablet around). **Attendance record
  PDF** downloads that day's sheet with every signature; it needs [Gotenberg](/en/docs/gotenberg).
- **Let them sign on their phones**: with the attendees and the date chosen, instead of passing the tablet
  around. It shows a **QR code** (and **Copy the link**) for that signing. Each attendee scans it, signs in
  with their username (or **Sign in with PIN**), sees the course, the date and who gave it, and signs with a
  finger. Only the people on the list can sign, each for themselves and once. Whoever gave it sees live who
  signed and who's missing, and can **Close the signing** whenever they want; otherwise it closes on its own
  after 24 hours. **Show the QR code** shows it again while it's open. The signatures go into the same day's
  **Attendance record PDF**, wherever they were signed.
- Each course shows every person with their state (**Up to date**, **Due soon**, **Expired**, **Pending**),
  and each person's sheet shows their training.

## Risk assessments

Turning the module on creates the **Risk assessment** document type (EVR, reviewed every 12 months). When an
EVR is published, its review date is told to the heads and deputies of its owner department (or whoever made
it, without one) 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day.

## Fitness for work

On each person's sheet, whoever has **Health data: accidents and fitness for work** records their **Fitness
for work**: **Fit**, **Fit with restrictions** (with the tasks they can't do, never the causes) or **Not fit**,
and until when it's valid. Never medical data. Each person sees their own. Before it expires the person and
whoever recorded it are told; the earlier one stays in the history.

With that capability, the sheet also shows the person's **Accidents and incidents**.

## Contractors (CAE)

**Coordination of business activities** (CAE: Law 31/1995, art. 24, and Royal Decree 171/2004 in Spain; in
Latin America, each country's equivalent duty) tells you which external companies work at the site, whether
their documents and each worker's are up to date, and whether they can come in today. It lives inside the
module, in the **Contractors (CAE)** tab.

**Turn it on.** The owner turns it on in **Administration** › **Security** › **General**, below the module,
in **Coordination of business activities (CAE)**. It needs the module on; turning the module off hides it
too. The first time, the **Contractors** folder is created in the module's space, along with your country's
**catalog of required documents**. It never overwrites what you already have with the same name. Turning it
off hides the tab and deletes nothing.

**Who sees what.** Two new capabilities in [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions):

- **Manage contractors (CAE)**: adds and changes companies, workers and the catalog; blocks and archives.
  The owner and the Administrator have it by default.
- **Check whether a contractor can come in**: only sees whether a company or a worker is authorized and why,
  without contact details or documents; of each worker's ID, only the last 3 characters. Meant for the
  gatehouse and team leads. Nobody has it by default except whoever manages contractors.

Each company's **internal contact** (a person in your company) sees its whole record even without those
capabilities.

**Companies.** The list shows each company with its type, its internal contact, how many workers it has and
its status: **Authorized** (green), **Incomplete** (amber, with the first reason), **Blocked** (red) or
**Archived**. Search by name or tax ID and filter by type and status. **New company** asks for the name, the
type (**Contractor**, **Subcontractor**, which works for a contractor, or **Concurrent company**, which shares
the site without a contract with your company), what it does at the site, the internal contact, the contact
details and, if it works for a while, the start and end dates. Each company gets its own folder inside
**Contractors**, named after it; renaming the company renames the folder.

**The company's record** says in green whether it can work at the site or, in red, every reason it can't
(“Missing: …”, “Company blocked: …”, “Stopped working on …”). Below are its details, the link to its folder,
the documents the catalog asks of it by its type, and its workers. **Block** asks for a reason; **Archive**
takes it off the lists without deleting anything (its workers and documents are kept). A subcontractor is
only authorized if its contractor is too.

**Workers.** In the company's record, **Add worker** asks for the name, the ID document (optional) and the
job or task. Contractors' workers aren't members of your company: they have no account and take no seat.
Don't store health data: for medical fitness only the fact that the certificate was presented is recorded.
Each worker's record says whether they can come in and why, and they can be edited, blocked or archived.

**Required documents.** The catalog (the **Required documents** tab inside Contractors) has the company's
documents and each worker's, in order (arrows to move them up or down). Each one says how long it's valid
(to suggest the expiry date), which companies it's asked of and whether it's mandatory: a missing mandatory
one keeps them from working at the site. They can be edited, added and archived.

**Upload and review documents.** In the company's (or worker's) record, each required document shows its
status: **Up to date**, **Due soon** (expires within 30 days), **Expired**, **To review**, **Rejected** or
**Missing**. **Upload** asks for the file and the expiry date (suggested from the document's validity) and
uploads it to the company's folder; a worker's go into a folder with their name inside the company's, created
with their first document. Uploading needs edit access to the module's space. If you've already reviewed it,
tick **Accept it now**; otherwise it's left **To review**.

- **Accept** makes it the current one (you can correct the expiry date); the previous one stays in the
  **History**. With an expiry date, the internal contact gets the notices 30 days before, 7 days before and
  on the day, like any other [expiry](/en/docs/expiries).
- **Reject** asks for a reason, shown next to the document.
- The file's name opens it in another tab; seeing it needs access to the folder, like any file.

A mandatory document only counts if it's accepted and hasn't expired. The **To review** tab gathers the
unreviewed documents of every company, oldest first, and their number shows on the **Contractors (CAE)**
tab; the list of companies shows how many each one has.

**Gatehouse.** The **Gatehouse** tab (inside Contractors) is for whoever has **Check whether a contractor
can come in** (whoever manages contractors sees it too), and works on the entrance's phone or tablet. Type
the name, the last characters of the ID or the company's name, tap the person and the answer shows up large:
green **Can come in** or red **Can't come in**, with every reason. It only shows the name, the company, the
job and the last 3 characters of the ID: never the documents. Whoever only has that capability goes straight
to the gatehouse when opening the module and doesn't see PPE, training or accidents. Checks with a negative
answer go into the audit log; positive ones don't.

**Worker's QR.** On each worker's record, **QR card** shows their card (name, company and QR) to print it.
The QR opens the gatehouse with that person chosen; seeing it needs signing in to SpiderFolio with
permission to check contractors, so the code is no use outside the company.

**Entry and exit log** (optional). The owner turns it on in **Administration** › **Security** › **General**,
below CAE, with **Record entries and exits at the gate**. With the log on, the gatehouse answer has **Record
entry** and **Record exit**; someone who isn't authorized can't be checked in. The **Accesses** tab shows a
day's entries and exits (today, unless you choose another) and can be filtered by company; each company's
record shows its **Today's accesses**.

**Authorized today (PDF).** On the company's record, **Authorized today (PDF)** downloads a PDF with the
company, the day, its status and each worker with whether they can come in and why not. The installation
has to generate PDFs ([Gotenberg](/en/docs/gotenberg)).

**Information for contractors.** The company that owns the site has to inform each external company of the
site's risks, the emergency measures and its instructions (Royal Decree 171/2004, articles 7 to 9). In
**Required documents**, the **Information for contractors** card chooses which controlled documents in force
are that information. With **Require it to authorize** ticked (it is by default), a company that hasn't
received the version in force of each one isn't authorized, with the reason “The site information hasn't
been handed over: …”. If you publish a new version of a document, it has to be handed over again.

On each company's record, **Site information** shows what it received and what it's missing. **Record a
handover** asks which documents are handed over, who receives them for the external company, the date and,
if possible, their signature on screen. Each handover stays in the list with its **Receipt (PDF)**, with the
documents and their version, the statement of whoever receives it and their signature.

**Work permits.** The **Permits** tab gathers the permits for jobs with special risks: **Hot work**, **Work
at height**, **Confined spaces**, **Electrical work** or **Other**. They apply to a contractor's workers and
also to own staff. **New permit** asks for the type, the external company (empty if only own staff do it),
the location, the work, from and until when it's valid (a few days at most: for longer, issue another one),
the people and the **precautions**, suggested by type and editable. Only authorized workers can be added.
The permit starts as a **Draft**; **Approve and activate** asks you to tick each precaution as checked and
to sign. Once approved, it's valid between its dates (**Scheduled**, **Active**, **Expired**). It's
**closed** when the work ends, with notes, or **cancelled** with a reason. Each permit downloads as a PDF
with the signature of whoever approved it, and the gatehouse shows each worker's active permits. Whoever
manages contractors issues them; whoever checks contractors sees them.

## Food industry examples

If you tick **Load food industry examples** when turning the module on, example positions are added, a PPE
catalog (cut-resistant gloves, safety footwear, cold room jacket, hearing protection, glasses, chain mail
apron) and the **Monthly safety inspection** form. It never overwrites what you already have with the same
name.
