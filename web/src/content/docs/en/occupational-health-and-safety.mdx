## What it's for

**Health & safety** (PRL in Spain, SST in Latin America) is the area of the app that gathers what occupational
risk prevention asks of a small company, without loose spreadsheets or parallel folders. It builds on what you
already use in the rest of the app: [positions](/en/docs/departments#positions),
[controlled documents](/en/docs/controlled-documents) and [forms](/en/docs/forms).

| Part                                                      | What for                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [PPE](/en/docs/ppe)                                       | Catalog, signed handouts with their receipt, replacement and what each position lacks.      |
| [Training and fitness](/en/docs/training-and-fitness)     | Courses with records and signatures, risk assessments and each person's fitness.            |
| [Accidents and rates](/en/docs/accidents-and-rates)       | Report and investigate cases, and the frequency, severity and incidence rates.              |
| [Preventive planning](/en/docs/preventive-planning)       | Every measure of the company with owner, date, priority and cost.                           |
| [Emergencies and drills](/en/docs/emergencies-and-drills) | Emergency plan, team with its training and the drills log.                                  |
| [Safety committee](/en/docs/safety-committee)             | Delegates, terms, meetings, minutes and agreements.                                         |
| [Contractors (CAE)](/en/docs/contractors-cae)             | External companies, their workers, their documents and work permits.                        |
| [Gatehouse and access](/en/docs/gatehouse-and-access)     | Know at the entrance whether someone can come in, with their QR, and log entries and exits. |
| [Contractors' portal](/en/docs/contractors-portal)        | Each contractor uploads its own documents with a link and a code, without an account.       |

## Turn it on

The area is off by default. The owner turns it on in **Administration** › **Security** › **General**, under
**Occupational health and safety** (see [Security and two-step verification](/en/docs/security-and-2fa)):

1. Tick **Occupational health and safety**.
2. Choose the **Terms**: **Spain: PRL and EPI** or **Latin America: SST and EPP**. In English the app always
   says PPE.
3. If you like, tick **Load food industry examples** (see [below](#food-industry-examples)).
4. Save.

Turning it on creates the area's space, the **Risk assessment** (EVR) and **Emergency plan** (PEM) document
types and the **Accident or incident** form. It never overwrites what you already have with the same name.

**Contractors (CAE)** and the **entry and exit log** have their own switch below, because not every company
needs them (see [Contractors](/en/docs/contractors-cae#turn-it-on)).

Turning the area off hides it, with its notices, without deleting anything.

## How it's organized

With the area on, **Health & safety** shows in the **areas rail** on the left (see
[Home](/en/docs/home#getting-around-the-areas)). Inside, the area's menu has:

- **Summary**, **People**, **PPE**, **Catalog**, **Training**, **Accidents**, **Planning**, **Rates**,
  **Emergencies**, **Committee** and **Contractors**;
- **Mine**: **My sheet**, **My measures** and **Report an accident**;
- the **Area documents**.

Everyone sees only the sections that apply to them, and the rail shows a number with what's pending: for whoever
manages it, the Summary's total; for everyone else, the PPE they lack, their pending training and their
measures.

Whoever manages the area opens it on the **Summary**: cards with what needs attention (people missing PPE, PPE
to replace, training expired or due soon and, with **Health data**, fitness due, open measures and open cases),
each with **See the list**.

Whoever doesn't manage the area goes straight to their own sheet. If they lack PPE or have training pending,
they also see it on [Home](/en/docs/home).

## Who sees what

Access comes from capabilities given in [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions), from each
department's heads and from who sits on the committee:

| Who                                             | What they see                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage occupational health and safety**       | The whole area except health data. The Admin role already has it.                              |
| **Health data: accidents and fitness for work** | Accident cases, each person's fitness and the rates by department. By default, only the owner. |
| **Manage contractors (CAE)**                    | Contractors, their workers, documents and work permits.                                        |
| **Check whether a contractor can come in**      | Only the gatehouse: whether someone can come in and why, without documents.                    |
| Heads and deputies                              | **People**, **PPE** and **Training** for their area (and sub-areas).                           |
| Committee members                               | **Committee**, read only.                                                                      |
| Everyone                                        | Their sheet, their PPE, their training, their measures and **Report an accident**.             |

Health data is never shown just for having **Manage occupational health and safety**: whoever needs it gets that
capability separately.

## Food industry examples

If you tick **Load food industry examples** when turning the area on, this is added:

- example positions: production, cold room and dispatch operator, maintenance technician and shift supervisor;
- a PPE catalog: cut-resistant gloves, safety footwear, cold room jacket, hearing protection, glasses and chain
  mail apron;
- the **Monthly safety inspection** form.

If you tick the same option when turning **CAE** on, the catalog of required documents adds the industry's: the
pest control company's certificate and, for each worker, food handling training and the site's hygiene rules.

It never overwrites what you already have with the same name, and everything can be edited or archived.
