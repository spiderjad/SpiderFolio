## What it's for

At the site entrance you need a single answer: **can this person come in?** The **Gatehouse** tab, inside
[Contractors](/en/docs/contractors-cae), gives it large, made for the entrance's phone or tablet.

## Who uses it

- Whoever has **Check whether a contractor can come in** (see [Roles and
  permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions)): the gatehouse, security, team leads. Opening Health & safety
  takes them straight to the gatehouse, and they don't see PPE, training or accidents.
- Whoever manages contractors sees it too.

## Check a person

1. Type the name, the last characters of the ID or the company's name.
2. Tap the person.
3. The answer shows up large: green **Can come in** or red **Can't come in**, with **every** reason (an expired
   document, the company blocked, the site information not handed over…).

It only shows the name, the company, the job, the last 3 characters of the ID and their active work permits:
**never the documents**. Checks with a negative answer go into the audit log; positive ones don't.

## Worker's QR

On each worker's record, **QR card** shows their card (name, company and QR) ready to print. At the entrance,
scanning the QR opens the gatehouse with that person already chosen.

Seeing the answer needs signing in to SpiderFolio with permission to check contractors: the code is no use
outside the company and shows nothing to someone who scans it without that permission.

## Entry and exit log

It's optional. The owner turns it on in **Administration** › **Security** › **General**, below CAE, with
**Record entries and exits at the gate**.

- The gatehouse answer adds **Record entry** and **Record exit**. Someone who isn't authorized can't be checked
  in.
- The **Accesses** tab shows a day's entries and exits (today, unless you choose another) and filters by
  company: it tells you who's inside in an emergency.
- Each company's record shows its **Today's accesses**.
