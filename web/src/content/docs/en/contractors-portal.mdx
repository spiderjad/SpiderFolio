## What it's for

Asking for documents by email and uploading them by hand takes time. With the **portal**, each external company
uploads its own: its documents, its workers' and the changes in its staff. You only review. It needs no
SpiderFolio account and takes no seat.

## Give a contractor access

Whoever has **Manage contractors (CAE)** does it, from the company's record in
[Contractors](/en/docs/contractors-cae):

1. On the **Portal access** card, click **Give access**.
2. Choose how many **days** it lasts (up to 180) and, if you like, the person's email, to remember who it was
   given to.
3. A **link** and a **code** are created. **The code is shown only once**: copy it now.
4. Give the contractor's person the link and the code **through different channels**: for example, the link by
   email and the code by phone or in person.

The card shows who gave it, when it expires and when it was last used.

- **Revoke** cuts it off at once.
- **Give a new code** revokes the previous one and creates another (for example, if the code was lost).
- After several wrong codes in a row the access **locks** and a new one has to be given.

## What the contractor sees

The contractor's person opens the link, types the code and clicks **Sign in**. They see **only their company**,
with your company's name, their internal contact and until when the access is valid. At the top, whether their
company can work at the site or, if not, why.

| Tab                  | What they can do                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Company**          | See the documents asked of them and their status, with the reason if one was rejected, and **Upload** each one (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 20 MB) with its expiry. |
| **Workers**          | Add, edit or **remove** workers, and upload each one's documents.                                                                                              |
| **Site information** | Open and read the site's documents (risks, emergencies, rules) and see which were already handed over.                                                         |
| **Work permits**     | See their company's permits, read only.                                                                                                                        |

They never see other companies, nor your company's files or data beyond their internal contact's name. Their
session lasts a few hours and ends with **Sign out**.

## What reaches your company

- Each uploaded document arrives **To review**, marked “Uploaded by the contractor”, in the
  [Contractors](/en/docs/contractors-cae#upload-and-review-documents) inbox. It doesn't count towards
  authorization until you accept it.
- The company's internal contact gets a notice in the bell.
- New workers show on the company's record; removed ones no longer appear at the site.

## Security

- The access stops working at once if it's **revoked** or **expires**, if the company is **archived** or if CAE
  is turned off.
- Only a hash of the code is stored, never the code: not even whoever gave it can see it again.
- Each sign-in, failed attempt and change goes into the [audit log](/en/docs/security-and-2fa#the-audit-log) as
  “Contractors' portal”.
