## What it's for

IFS Food 8 (1.2) and BRCGS 9 (1.1.2) ask for a food safety culture plan: objectives, activities that are
followed up and a way to measure how it's going. In the Quality area, **Culture** brings together each year's
plan and the surveys.

<Shot
  name="calidad-cultura"
  alt="The year's culture plan: the objectives, the activities with their date and status, and the surveys"
/>

The arrows at the top change the year.

## Objectives and activities

Whoever manages quality writes the **year's objectives** with **Edit objectives** and adds activities with
**New activity**: the **type** (training, communication, survey, meeting or other), the **planned date**, the
**responsible** person and a note.

The responsible person gets the reminders 7 days before, the day before and on the day, until they mark it
**Done** with the date it was done. If it was marked by mistake, **Back to pending** reopens it. An activity
past its date without being done shows as **Overdue**.

## Anonymous surveys

**New survey** asks for a title and the dates it's open. The **statements** are answered from 1 (strongly
disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). If you don't write any, the survey gets ten statements on management
commitment, communication, training, responsibility and reporting problems without fear. They can be changed
until the first answer comes in; after that, the survey can only be closed.

While it's open, everyone in the company (not guests) sees it in **My to-dos** and answers it once, with an
optional comment.

<Shot
  name="calidad-encuesta"
  alt="Answering a survey: each statement with its 1 to 5 scale and the optional comment"
/>

### Anonymity

- The answer is stored **without the person and without the date**. Who has already answered is stored
  separately, with nothing linking it to what they answered.
- Neither the audit log nor the notifications say what anyone answered.
- **Results** show from **5 answers**: with fewer, only the turnout shows.
- Comments are shown in random order. It's worth reminding people not to put their name.

## Results

**See results** shows the **turnout** (answers over the company's active people), the **average** from 1 to 5
and, for each statement, its average and how the answers were spread, and the comments.

<Shot
  name="calidad-encuesta-resultados"
  alt="A survey's results: turnout, average, the spread of each statement and the comments"
/>

The Quality **Summary** shows the overdue activities and the last survey's turnout and average.

## Who sees what

| Who                       | What they see and do                            |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality**        | Everything: objectives, activities and surveys. |
| **View quality**          | Reads the plan and the survey results.          |
| An activity's responsible | Sees theirs and marks them done.                |
| Anyone (not a guest)      | Answers the open surveys from **My to-dos**.    |
