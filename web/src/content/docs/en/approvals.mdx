## What they're for

Before a version of a [controlled document](/en/docs/controlled-documents) becomes the current one, someone with authority has to review and approve it. Everything is recorded in the document's history and in the audit log.

## Who can approve

- The owner and admins.
- Anyone whose role has the **Approve documents** capability.
- The head or deputy of the document's responsible department, or of one of its parent departments.

They also have to be able to **see** the document: nobody approves what they can't see. Whoever sends a document can't approve it, unless the owner turns on **Allow self-approval** in **Administration** › **Security**; in that case it's recorded as "approved (the author)".

## Send for review

You need to be able to **edit** the document, and its newest version must be a **Draft**.

1. Open **Document control** (on a page, from **More actions**; on a file, from its menu) and click **Send for review**.
2. Check the **Responsible department**. If needed, click **Change department**: its head is the suggested approver.
3. Under **Who approves**, check the people. Each one shows why they can approve ("Head of Quality", "Admin", "Can approve"). If you choose several people, all of them must approve.
4. If someone can't see the document, they show as "Doesn’t have access to this document". If you manage access, click **Give access to review** to give them read-only access.
5. Write one line for the reviewer in **What changed (optional)** and click **Send for review**.

While it's in review, the content is frozen: nobody can edit it. If you need to change something, **Withdraw from review** sends it back to draft (the approvals for that submission are lost) and you can send it again. Whoever sent it, or whoever manages the document, can withdraw it.

## The Approvals inbox

If you can approve documents, the side menu has **Approvals**: the documents waiting for your approval, with their code, version and who sent them. They also show up in **My to-dos** on Home, and you get a notification ("Pending approval: “…”").

Click **Review** to open the document. On a page, reviewers see the version in review, not the current one. For a file, the inbox takes you to its space: open it from its menu.

## Review and decide

1. Read the document. To comment on the text, select a passage and use **Comment** (see [Pages](/en/docs/pages)); the content stays frozen.
2. Open **Document control** and choose:
   - **Approve**: you can leave an optional comment. If you're the last person left, the document becomes **Approved** and ready to publish.
   - **Request changes**: write the **Reason**. It goes back to draft with your comments open, and whoever sent it is notified. They can fix it and send it again.
   - **Reject**: write the **Reason**. The version is discarded and stays in the history: it isn't published. If there was a current version, it stays the same.

The dialog reminds you how many comments you opened and resolved. If you already reviewed an earlier round, it shows the **Changes since your review** so you don't have to read everything again.

Whoever sent the document gets a notification for each decision (approved, changes requested, rejected).

## After approval

An approved version isn't current yet: it has to be **Published**. Whoever can approve the document does this from **Document control**. See [Publish](/en/docs/controlled-documents#publish).

## All reviews in the company

In **Administration** › **Controlled documents**, the **Approvals** tab lists everything in review, with each approver's state (**approved**, **requested changes**, **rejected** or **pending**).
