## What it's for

**My documents** is your personal list: what you need to read and what changed in your area, without having to look for it in folders. It's in the side menu, below **Home**. People with a reader seat land here when they sign in.

<Shot name="mis-documentos" alt="My documents with pending readings, what's new in the area and recent documents" />

## To read

The [required readings](/en/docs/required-reading) you still have to do, for the current version of each document. Each card shows the code, the version and the due date ("Due: …", or "Was due …" in red if it has passed). The number next to the heading tells you how many you have.

1. Click **Open** to read the document.
2. Once you've read it, click **I have read and understood**. The reading disappears from the list.

If you have nothing pending, you'll see **All caught up**.

## New in your area this week

Versions published this week of documents whose responsible department is one of yours (or a parent area), as long as you can see them. It keeps you informed about new or updated procedures even if nobody asked you to read them.

## Favorites and Recent

- **Favorites**: the spaces you starred in [Files](/en/docs/files).
- **Recent**: the last documents (pages and files) you opened. It's private: nobody else sees your visits.
