## What it's for

**Coordination of business activities** (CAE: Law 31/1995, art. 24, and Royal Decree 171/2004 in Spain; in
Latin America, each country's equivalent duty) answers three questions:

- which external companies work at the site?
- are their documents and each worker's up to date?
- can they come in today?

It lives inside Health & safety, in the **Contractors** section. This guide is for whoever manages it. The site
entrance is in [Gatehouse and access](/en/docs/gatehouse-and-access) and what the contractor sees, in
[Contractors' portal](/en/docs/contractors-portal).

## Turn it on

1. With [Health & safety](/en/docs/occupational-health-and-safety#turn-it-on) on, the owner goes to
   **Administration** › **Security** › **General**.
2. Below the area, tick **Coordination of business activities (CAE)**.
3. If you like, also tick **Load food industry examples**.
4. Save.

The first time, the **Contractors** folder is created in the Health & safety space, along with your country's
**catalog of required documents**. It never overwrites what you already have with the same name. Turning Health
& safety off turns CAE off too, and turning CAE off hides the section without deleting anything.

## Who sees what

Two capabilities in [Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions):

| Capability                                 | What it allows                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage contractors (CAE)**               | Add and change companies, workers and the catalog; review documents; issue permits; block and archive. By default, the owner and the Administrator.                        |
| **Check whether a contractor can come in** | Only the [gatehouse](/en/docs/gatehouse-and-access): whether a company or a worker is authorized and why, without contacts or documents. For the gatehouse and team leads. |

Each company's **internal contact** (a person in your company) sees its whole record even without those
capabilities, and gets its notices.

## Companies

The list shows each company with its type, its internal contact, how many workers it has and its status:

- **Authorized** (green): it can work at the site.
- **Incomplete** (amber): something's missing; the first reason shows.
- **Blocked** (red): someone blocked it, with a reason.
- **Archived**: it no longer works with your company.

Search by name or tax ID and filter by type and status.

**Add a company.** Click **New company** and fill in:

- the name and tax ID;
- the **type**: **Contractor**, **Subcontractor** (works for a contractor) or **Concurrent company** (shares
  the site without a contract with your company);
- what it does at the site, the **internal contact** and the contact details;
- if it works for a while, the start and end dates.

Each company gets its own folder inside **Contractors**, named after it; renaming the company renames the
folder.

## The company's record

At the top it says in green whether it can work at the site or, in red, **every** reason it can't (“Missing:
…”, “Company blocked: …”, “Stopped working on …”). Below are its details, the link to its folder, its documents,
the site information, its workers, its portal access and today's accesses.

- **Block** asks for a reason; **Unblock** undoes it.
- **Archive** takes it off the lists without deleting anything (its workers and documents are kept).
- A subcontractor is only authorized if its contractor is too.
- **Authorized today (PDF)** downloads a PDF with the company, the day, its status and each worker with whether
  they can come in and why not. It needs [Gotenberg](/en/docs/gotenberg).

## Workers

In the company's record, **Add worker** asks for the name, the ID document (optional) and the job or task.

- Contractors' workers **aren't members** of your company: they have no account and take no seat.
- **Don't store health data**: for medical fitness only the fact that the certificate was presented is
  recorded.
- Each worker's record says whether they can come in and why, with their documents, and they can be edited,
  blocked or archived. Their [QR card](/en/docs/gatehouse-and-access#workers-qr) is there too.

## Required documents

The catalog, in the **Required documents** tab, has the documents asked of each company and each worker. For
each one you decide:

- **how long it's valid**, to suggest the expiry date when uploading;
- **which companies** it's asked of: all, contractors and subcontractors, or concurrent companies only;
- whether it's **mandatory**: a missing mandatory one keeps them from working at the site.

Reorder them with the arrows, edit, add and archive them.

## Upload and review documents

In the company's (or worker's) record, each required document shows its status: **Up to date**, **Due soon**
(expires within 30 days), **Expired**, **To review**, **Rejected** or **Missing**.

1. Click **Upload** on the document.
2. Choose the file and the expiry date (suggested from the document's validity).
3. If you've already reviewed it, tick **Accept it now**; otherwise it's left **To review**.

The file goes into the company's folder; a worker's go into a folder with their name inside the company's,
created with their first document. Uploading needs edit access to the Health & safety space. The contractor can
also upload them itself from the [portal](/en/docs/contractors-portal).

**Review.** The **To review** tab gathers the unreviewed documents of every company, oldest first; their number
shows on the **Contractors** tab and the list of companies shows how many each one has.

- **Accept** makes it the current one (you can correct the expiry date); the previous one stays in the
  **History**. With an expiry date, the internal contact gets the notices 30 days before, 7 days before and on
  the day, like any other [expiry](/en/docs/expiries).
- **Reject** asks for a reason, shown next to the document (in the contractor's portal too).
- The file's name opens it in another tab; seeing it needs access to the folder, like any file.

A mandatory document only counts if it's **accepted and hasn't expired**.

## Site information

The company that owns the site has to inform each external company of the site's risks, the emergency
measures and its instructions (Royal Decree 171/2004, articles 7 to 9).

- **Choose what's handed over.** In **Required documents**, the **Information for contractors** card chooses
  which controlled documents in force are that information (for example, the [emergency
  plan](/en/docs/emergencies-and-drills) and the site rules).
- **Require it.** With **Require it to authorize** ticked (it is by default), a company that hasn't received the
  version in force of each one isn't authorized, with the reason “The site information hasn't been handed over:
  …”. If you publish a new version of a document, it has to be handed over again.
- **Record the handover.** On each company's record, **Site information** shows what it received and what it's
  missing. **Record a handover** asks which documents are handed over, who receives them for the external
  company, the date and, if possible, their signature on screen. Each handover keeps its **Receipt (PDF)**: the
  documents with their version, the statement of whoever receives it and their signature.

## Work permits

The **Permits** tab gathers the permits for jobs with special risks: **Hot work**, **Work at height**,
**Confined spaces**, **Electrical work** or **Other**. They apply to a contractor's workers and also to own
staff.

1. Click **New permit** and choose the type, the external company (empty if only own staff do it), the location
   and the work.
2. Set from and until when it's valid: a few days at most; for longer, issue another one.
3. Add the people. Only authorized workers can be added.
4. Check the **precautions**, suggested by type and editable.
5. The permit starts as a **Draft**. **Approve and activate** asks you to tick each precaution as checked and to
   sign.

Once approved, it's valid between its dates (**Scheduled**, **Active**, **Expired**). It's **closed** when the
work ends, with notes, or **cancelled** with a reason. Each permit downloads as a PDF with the signature of
whoever approved it, and the gatehouse shows each worker's active permits. Whoever manages contractors issues
them; whoever checks contractors sees them.
