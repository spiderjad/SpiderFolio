## What it's for

IFS Food 8 and BRCGS 9 ask to approve each supplier according to the risk of what it supplies, to keep its
certificates and technical sheets in force, to review it every year and to assess the fraud vulnerability of
each raw material. **Suppliers** (Quality menu) brings all of that together.

## The register

The list shows each supplier with its category, the **risk** of what it supplies, its **status** (**Pending**,
**Approved**, **Conditional** or **Blocked**), the worst state of its **certificates** (**Valid**, **Due soon**
or **Expired**) and its **next review**, in red with “Review due” once it's passed. Search by name, tax ID or
what it supplies, and filter by status, category and risk.

<Shot
  name="calidad-proveedores"
  alt="The supplier register with their risk, status, certificates and next review"
/>

**New supplier** (whoever manages quality) asks for the name, the tax ID, the **category** (raw material,
ingredient and additive, packaging, service or other), **what it supplies**, its **risk**, the **fraud
vulnerability** with its assessment (for example, paprika and dyes), the contact and **who follows it**. It's
**Pending** until its first review.

## The record

<Shot
  name="calidad-proveedor"
  alt="A conditional spice supplier, with a certificate due soon and its review"
/>

- **Documents**: certificates (IFS, BRCGS or others), technical sheets, approval questionnaires and analyses,
  each with its **expiry**. Whoever follows the supplier is told 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day,
  also in [Expiries](/en/docs/expiries). A document can point to a file in the supplier's folder: **Open the
  folder** creates it inside **Suppliers** in the Quality space; upload the PDF there and choose it when adding
  the document. A document that no longer applies is **archived**.
- **Reviews**: **Review** asks for the date, a score from 1 to 5 for quality, service, documents and
  incidents, the **result** (**Approved**, **Conditional** or **Blocked**) and its grounds. The supplier moves
  to that status and its next review is set **12 months** later (**6** if conditional), with its notice. While
  reviewing you see how many open nonconformities it has.
- **Nonconformities**: its own. **Open nonconformity** creates one with origin **Supplier**, linked to it; the
  NC's record links the supplier.

**Archive** takes the supplier off the list (filter **Archived** to see it) without deleting anything.

## Who sees what

| Who                        | What they see and do                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Manage quality**         | Everything: adds, edits, reviews, loads documents, opens NCs and archives.                 |
| **View quality**           | Reads the register and each record.                                                        |
| Whoever follows a supplier | Sees it even without capabilities and loads and archives its documents; doesn't review it. |
