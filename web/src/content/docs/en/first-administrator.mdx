With SpiderFolio installed ([Install on your server](/en/docs/install)), you need the first person: the
one who creates the company and becomes its **owner**. This guide covers that first day.

## 1. Create the company

1. Open your installation's address followed by `/register` (for example,
   `https://docs.your-company.com/register`). You can also get there from the sign-in screen with
   **Get started for free**.
2. Fill in **Full name**, **Company name**, **Email** and **Password** (at least 10 characters).
3. Tick **I accept the Terms and conditions and the Privacy policy** and click **Create account**.

Whoever signs up creates the company and gets the **Owner** role: they can do everything, including the
**Security** section, which only the owner can manage.

One installation can host several companies, each with its data isolated. For now, sign-up stays open to
anyone who reaches `/register`: if your server is only for your company, don't publish it outside your
network, or keep an eye on new companies from the back office (step 4).

## 2. Confirm your email

You'll get an email with a link. Until you open it, you can't invite your team or publish documents,
and Home reminds you with a banner. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and the server's email
settings ([Troubleshooting](/en/docs/troubleshooting)).

## 3. Turn on two-step verification

You're the most powerful account in the company: protect it with a second factor.

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My profile**.
2. Under **Two-step verification**, click **Turn on**.
3. Scan the code with your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or another).
   If you can't scan it, click **Can't scan it? Show the key** and type it in.
4. Enter the 6-digit code shown in the app and click **Turn on**.
5. Save **Your recovery codes** (**Copy** or **Download**) somewhere safe and click **I've saved them**.
   Each code works only once and they won't be shown again.

More in [Security and two-step verification](/en/docs/security-and-2fa).

## 4. Grant access to the server back office

The back office (`/internal`, **Companies on this installation**) is for whoever manages the server: it
lists every company on the installation with its usage and limits. Counts only, never content.

There's no button for it: you flag the person in the database. Their account must already exist. From
the server's `deploy/` folder:

```sh
docker compose exec db psql -U spiderfolio -d spiderfolio \
  -c "UPDATE \"User\" SET \"isStaff\" = true WHERE email = 'you@example.com';"
```

If you changed `POSTGRES_USER` or `POSTGRES_DB` in `.env`, use those values for `-U` and `-d`.

- The person must have **two-step verification turned on**: without it, the back office won't open.
- For everyone else, `/internal` doesn't exist (it responds like a page that can't be found).
- To remove access, run the same command with `false`. It takes effect immediately.

It's an installation-level permission, not a company one: the person doesn't need to be a member of
any company.

## 5. Recommended first steps

1. **Review the company's security**: in **Management › Security**, choose the company's **Language** and **Time zone**,
   **People visibility**, whether controlled documents allow self-approval and what department heads
   can do.

   <Shot name="seguridad" alt="The company Security section, with the language, people visibility and controlled document rules" />

2. **Set up departments** in **Management › Departments**: permissions build on them
   ([Departments](/en/docs/departments)).
3. **Invite your team** from **Management › Members** ([Invite people](/en/docs/invite-people)). Give
   the admin role only to those who need it ([Roles and permissions](/en/docs/roles-and-permissions)).
4. **Set limits** if you want to cap storage or file size, in **Management › Limits and usage**
   ([Limits](/en/docs/limits)). There are none by default.
5. **Schedule backups** before you load real data ([Backups](/en/docs/backups)).
6. Optional: the company's own [SMTP email](/en/docs/smtp-email) and [Bian](/en/docs/bian-setup).
