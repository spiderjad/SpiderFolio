**Connector for Claude: editing** does everything the [Connector for Claude](/en/docs/connector-for-claude)
does (search and read) and also lets Claude **create and edit** documentation, always with the
permissions of the person who connected it:

- create pages from what you ask («Create a draft work instruction for the forklift in the Safety
  folder»), also as a **controlled document in draft** («…as a controlled procedure»): it gets its code
  (for example, PRC-004) and stays a draft, unpublished;
- edit pages: replace them, add at the end or change a piece;
- create folders and move pages, folders and files;
- upload small files (up to 10 MB);
- comment on a page;
- send the draft of a controlled document for review.

It's a **paid** plugin. It includes the free one: if you had the Connector for Claude, connections keep
working for reading. For Claude to edit too, each person disconnects SpiderFolio in Claude and connects it
again: the authorize screen has to say it asks to create and edit.

What Claude changes shows as "*name* via Claude" in the explorer's **Modified by**, in "Last edited" at the
foot of the page and in the audit log. Once someone edits it again in SpiderFolio, it shows just that
person.

## What it never does

Claude can't **publish or approve** documents, confirm readings on anyone's behalf, **delete** or trash,
or change permissions, members or settings. A person always does those in SpiderFolio.

In a **published controlled document**, an edit by Claude creates a **new draft**: the current version
doesn't change until someone approves and publishes it.

## 1. Install the plugin (the owner)

1. In **Administration › License and plugins**, copy the **Company ID** and send it to us
   ([contact](mailto:contacto@spiderjad.com)).
2. We send you a `.sfplugin` signed for your company. Upload it in **Upload plugin** and click **Restart
   now**.

**Administration › Security › Connector for Claude** then says «Claude can read and edit».

## 2. Connect Claude (each person)

Same as with the free one (the full steps, with screenshots, are in
[Connector for Claude](/en/docs/connector-for-claude#2-connect-claude-each-person)):

1. In Claude: **Customize › Connectors › Add custom connector**.
2. **Name**: `SpiderFolio`; **MCP server URL**: the one in **My profile › Connected apps** (for example,
   `https://docs.yourcompany.com/mcp`).
3. **Add** and **Connect**; in SpiderFolio pick the company and click **Allow**.

**On a real server, use its own address**: `https://your-domain/mcp`, that is, the installation's
`APP_URL` from its `.env` file with `/mcp` at the end (for example, `https://docs.yourcompany.com/mcp`).
Never `localhost` or an internal IP: Claude can't reach them. `https://….trycloudflare.com` addresses are
only for [testing the connector from a PC](/en/docs/test-the-connector) and change every time. If your
SpiderFolio's address changes, each person edits the connector in Claude and connects again.

The screen says what Claude will be able to do. People with a **reader** seat can only give reading, even
with the editing plugin installed.

**If it was already connected with the free plugin**, nothing needs doing: the same connection can now
edit.

## 3. Use it

Ask Claude as usual. For example:

- «Create in the Quality space a page "Forklift work instruction" with the daily check steps.»
- «Add a section about new suppliers at the end of the purchasing procedure.»
- «Move the slicer work instruction to the Plant folder.»
- «Comment on the cleaning procedure that the weekend frequency is missing.»
- «Send the PRC-002 draft for review.» (Claude asks you who approves it.)

Claude writes in **Markdown**: headings, lists, quotes, code, links, bold and italic. Images and HTML
aren't added; Claude tells you if it left something out.

**If someone changed the page in the meantime**, Claude saves nothing: it gets the current version and
asks you how to go on. That way it never overwrites anyone's work.

## If the license expires

Claude goes back to **reading** only, if you have the Connector for Claude (free) installed: connections
keep working. Without the free one, the connector stops answering until you load a new license.

## Common problems

- **Claude says it can't create or edit**: the person can't edit that folder or page, or has a reader
  seat. Claude can never do more than the person.
- **«Conflict»**: someone changed the page. Ask Claude to read it again and redo the change.
- **A file doesn't upload**: over 10 MB, a type the company doesn't allow, or the storage limit was
  reached. Upload it from SpiderFolio.
- **It can't edit a controlled document**: it's in review, or approved and waiting to be published. It can
  be edited when it's back to draft or after it's published.
- For the rest (it doesn't connect, «Couldn't reach the MCP server», «Too many calls»), see the common
  problems of the [Connector for Claude](/en/docs/connector-for-claude#common-problems).
