## What it's for

IFS Food 8 (4.2.1.3 and 2.3.1) and BRCGS 9 (1.1.11 and 2.3) ask for every change that may affect product
safety to be reviewed before it's made. In the Quality area, **Changes** brings together each change with its
review, the decision and its implementation. Each has its code, **CAM-YYYY-NNNN**, numbered within the year.

## Proposing a change

Anyone in the company (not guests) clicks **Propose a change** and says **what changes**, the **type**
(recipe, product, line, equipment, supplier, process or other), a description and, if they know it, the
**planned date**. Whoever views quality can also pick the **product affected**. Whoever manages quality gets
a notification.

Whoever proposed it follows it in the list and on its page, without being able to change it. If they don't
view all of quality, they find it under **Mine** › **Propose a change**.

## Reviewing and deciding

Whoever manages quality opens the change and completes the **review**: each point is **Pending**, **Not
applicable** or **Done**, with its note.

- HACCP hazards and CCPs.
- Allergens.
- Label and product sheet.
- Staff training.
- Validation and verification.

<Shot
  name="calidad-cambio"
  alt="A change's page: the description, the review point by point and the decision"
/>

With **Edit** they also choose who is **responsible for implementing it** and the food safety **impact** (no
impact, low or high).

- **Approve** needs the HACCP review answered.
- **Reject** needs the reason.

Whoever proposed it and the responsible person get a notification with the decision.

## Implementing it

Once approved and with a planned date, the responsible person gets the reminders 7 days before, the day before
and on the day. When no review point is left pending, they click **Mark implemented** with the date. An
approved change past its date shows as **Overdue**.

Until it's implemented, whoever manages quality can **Cancel the change**. Implemented, rejected or cancelled,
it stays as a record and no longer changes.

The Quality **Summary** shows the changes awaiting a decision and the approved ones overdue.

## Who sees what

| Who                           | What they see and do                                                   |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality**            | Everything: reviews, edits, approves, rejects, implements and cancels. |
| **View quality**              | Reads every change.                                                    |
| Whoever is responsible for it | Sees it and marks it implemented.                                      |
| Whoever proposed it           | Sees it, without changing it.                                          |
