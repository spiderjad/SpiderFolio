## What it's for

Spain's **Ley 2/2023** requires companies with **50 or more employees** to have an internal reporting system:
a channel to report breaches anonymously or confidentially, with an acknowledgement, an answer within a
deadline and protection against retaliation. SpiderFolio's whistleblowing channel covers it without separate
software.

- Anyone related to the company (staff, former employees, candidates, contractors, suppliers) reports from a
  **public page**, without an account.
- They can do it **anonymously**: SpiderFolio doesn't keep their address, their browser or their account.
- They follow their case with a **follow-up code** and a password, and write with management.
- Only the people who manage the channel see the cases. **Neither the owner nor the admins** see them unless
  they're designated.

## Turning it on

In **Administration › Security › General**, the **Whistleblowing channel** card. Only the **owner**, with
two-step verification on (the code is asked for when saving if needed).

1. In **Channel management**, add the people who will handle the cases. They need a **full seat** and
   **two-step verification** turned on. One of them is **responsible for the system**, the person the company
   designates before the authority for the protection of whistleblowers.
2. Turn on **Channel on**. It can't be turned on without a responsible person.
3. Copy the **public link** and put it on the home page of the company's website and on posters at the plant
   and in the changing rooms.
4. In **External channels**, write which authority applies to the company. If you leave it empty, a general
   text is shown.

## How someone reports

The public page (also in everyone's menu, **Whistleblowing channel**) explains what can be reported, who can
report, the guarantees and the external channels. **Report something** opens the form:

- **What it's about** (the category), **what happened** and, if they want, the people involved, when and where.
- **I want to identify myself**: if they don't tick it, the report is anonymous.
- If more than one person manages the channel, they can tick who **must not see** their report (for example,
  if it's about them). At least one has to be able to see it.

## The reporter's mailbox

In **I already reported: see my case**, with the code and the password, they see the case's status, when it was
received, the answer's deadline, the reason if it wasn't admitted or the outcome when it's closed, and the
messages with management, signed «The channel's management», without names. They can write from there.

<Shot
  name="canal-buzon"

Ten wrong passwords in a row lock the mailbox for 24 hours. The session ends by itself after 30 minutes without
use.

## Handling the cases

The people in management have the **Channel** area in the rail. **Cases** lists the reports they can see, with
their number, category, status and the deadline in color. The list doesn't show the content.

In each case:

- **Admit** or **Don't admit** (with the reason, shown in the mailbox).
- **Start the investigation** and **Close** with the outcome (substantiated, not substantiated, partly or
  referred to an authority) and a summary for the reporter.
- **Extend the deadline** by 3 months, only once and with a reason.
- Record that it was **referred to the public prosecutor** or that the **person concerned was informed**.
- Choose the **investigator**, write to the reporter and add **internal notes**.
- **Stand aside** from the case if there's a conflict of interest.

The case's **log of access and changes** says who opened or changed it and when. It doesn't go to the general
audit log: so nobody outside management knows the case exists.

## Deadlines and notices

- The **acknowledgement** is automatic, when it's received.
- The **answer** has to come within **3 months** of the acknowledgement (6 if extended). The people in
  management get notices 30 and 7 days before and on the day it's due.
- Notices never say the case's number or content: only that there's something new or that a case is due.

## Confidentiality

- Everything the reporter and management write is stored **encrypted**.
- The public page leaves no trace of the reporter in the server's logs, and the attempt limits don't keep
  their address.
- The channel stays out of Bian, the connector for Claude, search and the general exports.
