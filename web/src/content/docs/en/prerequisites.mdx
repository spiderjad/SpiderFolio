## What it's for

IFS Food 8 and BRCGS 9 ask to show the prerequisite programmes are met: cleaning, pest control, water,
calibration of measuring equipment, glass and hard plastic, waste. In the Quality area, **Prerequisites**
gathers those controls, each with how often it's due, who does it and when it was last done.

## Create a control

Whoever manages quality clicks **New control** and chooses:

- the **programme** (cleaning and disinfection, pest control, water, calibration, glass and hard plastic, waste
  or other) and **what is checked**;
- **where**, **every how many days** and the **next date**;
- the **responsible** person, who is told 7 days before, the day before and on the day;
- optionally, the **form** of the Quality space where the detail is written down.

Each control is **Up to date**, **Due soon** (7 days or less left) or **Overdue**. It's edited with the pencil
and archived with the last button; **Show archived** lists them.

## Record a check

The control's responsible person (or whoever manages quality) clicks **Record**, enters the date and whether it
was **Conforming** or **Not conforming**, with remarks. The next one is due on that date plus the control's
days.

If it wasn't conforming, **Open a nonconformity** creates it with origin **Internal** and the chosen severity,
in the hands of the control's responsible person (see [Nonconformities and CAPA](/en/docs/nonconformities)).
The clock opens each control's **history**, with its nonconformities.

## In the Summary

The area's **Summary** shows the overdue controls, those due in 7 days and the non-conforming checks of the last
30 days, with the figures of the rest of the module (see [Quality: getting started](/en/docs/quality)).

## Who sees what

| Who                     | What they see and do                                  |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manage quality**      | Everything: creates, edits, archives and records.     |
| **View quality**        | Reads every control and its history.                  |
| A control's responsible | Sees it in **Mine** › **My controls** and records it. |
