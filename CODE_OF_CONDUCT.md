# Code of conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We who take part in SpiderFolio pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We
pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## What we expect

- Empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect for differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.

## What we do not accept

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Derogatory comments, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information (addresses, emails…) without their explicit
  permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, forums) and when someone
represents the project in public. Cases are reported to contacto@spiderjad.com and are reviewed promptly and
confidentially. Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may moderate, warn,
suspend or ban anyone who does not respect this code.
